# CWIG-01 — Scope & Limitation Statement v0.1

**Prepared by:** Carpenters for Housing Reform Victoria  
**Status:** Working Draft — Controlled Pre-Pilot Governance v0.1  
**Date:** August 2026  
**Purpose:** Establish the canonical scope, responsibility boundary and limitations of the Critical Waterproofing Interface Gate (CWIG-01) for controlled pilot use.

---

## 1. Controlling Statement

> **CWIG-01 is a limited pre-waterproofing interface decision process. It records whether the observable and reasonably measurable interface presented at the time of assessment is suitable for the identified waterproofing system to proceed, subject to the evidence and technical basis available at that time.**

CWIG-01 does not certify the bathroom, building, waterproofing work, plumbing work, structural work, framing, lining or other upstream construction as compliant.

It is not:

- a statutory inspection;
- a building approval;
- a waterproofing compliance certificate;
- a plumbing certificate;
- an inspection or certification of concealed construction;
- certification of upstream trade work;
- government approval;
- government endorsement;
- a guarantee against future leakage or waterproofing failure.

CWIG-01 has no statutory or regulatory status.

---

## 2. What CWIG-01 Actually Assesses

CWIG-01 examines the waterproofing interface through six defined domains:

1. **CWI-01 — Substrate Acceptance**
2. **CWI-02 — Water-Management Geometry**
3. **CWI-03 — Drainage and Penetration Interface**
4. **CWI-04 — Junction and Movement Treatability**
5. **CWI-05 — Surface and Moisture Acceptance**
6. **CWI-06 — Waterproofing System Compatibility**

The assessment is limited to conditions that are:

- observable;
- reasonably measurable;
- relevant to the waterproofing interface;
- capable of being assessed within the participating practitioner's reasonable competence and role;
- material to the decision about whether the identified waterproofing system should proceed.

---

## 3. CWI-01 — Substrate Acceptance

CWI-01 applies an important responsibility boundary.

> **The practitioner assesses whether the substrate as presented is suitable for installation of the identified waterproofing system.**

The practitioner is not certifying that:

- framing was correctly designed;
- framing was correctly constructed;
- structural work is compliant;
- lining was correctly installed;
- concealed fixings are compliant;
- plumbing work is compliant;
- any other upstream practitioner correctly performed their work.

An upstream condition may nevertheless be material to CWIG where it affects whether the presented interface can reasonably be accepted for waterproofing.

CWIG therefore distinguishes between:

**who is responsible for the originating work**

and

**whether the resulting condition is material to the waterproofing decision.**

These are not the same question.

---

## 4. UNRESOLVED vs OUTSIDE CWIG SCOPE

CWIG-01 uses two different uncertainty classifications.

### UNRESOLVED — MATERIAL TO CWIG DECISION

Use this where the practitioner cannot make a defensible waterproofing-interface decision until the relevant condition is resolved.

The originating work may belong to another practitioner.

That does not make the waterproofing practitioner responsible for certifying that work.

It means only that the unresolved condition is material to the waterproofing decision.

### NOT VERIFIED — OUTSIDE CWIG SCOPE

Use this where a matter:

- is observed or identified;
- falls outside the reasonable scope or competence of CWIG;
- and does not need to be resolved for the practitioner to make the current waterproofing-interface decision.

### Controlling Rule

> **Trade responsibility does not determine CWIG decision relevance.**

If an apparently outside-scope condition becomes necessary to the waterproofing decision, it cannot remain classified merely as:

`NOT VERIFIED — OUTSIDE CWIG SCOPE`

It must be treated as:

`UNRESOLVED — MATERIAL TO CWIG DECISION`

or referred, investigated or escalated as appropriate.

---

## 5. CWIG Does Not Transfer Responsibility

Participation in CWIG does not remove, reduce or transfer the existing responsibilities of:

- builders;
- waterproofing practitioners;
- plumbers;
- carpenters;
- lining installers;
- tilers;
- designers;
- engineers;
- relevant building surveyors;
- manufacturers;
- other project participants.

A CWIG practitioner is responsible for the CWIG decision they make within their role.

They do not become responsible for another practitioner's work merely because that work creates or affects the waterproofing interface.

---

## 6. CWIG Is Not an Overall Compliance Assessment

CWIG may use or refer to applicable technical information including:

- the National Construction Code;
- relevant Australian Standards;
- project drawings or specifications;
- manufacturer system requirements;
- approved project-specific technical information.

That technical basis informs the interface decision.

It does not convert CWIG into an overall building-compliance assessment.

A CWIG assessment does not determine whether the entire bathroom, building or completed waterproofing installation complies with every applicable requirement.

---

## 7. Meaning of a Positive CWIG Decision

A decision of:

**PROCEED**

or

**PROCEED WITH CONTROL**

means only that the current CWIG assessment supports proceeding under the recorded conditions and technical basis.

It does not mean:

- the bathroom is certified compliant;
- completed waterproofing work is certified compliant;
- plumbing is certified compliant;
- framing or lining is certified compliant;
- all concealed work has been inspected;
- no defect exists;
- no future defect can occur;
- no leak can occur;
- an independent statutory inspection has occurred;
- government has approved the work;
- another practitioner has been released from responsibility.

---

## 8. Meaning of PROCEED WITH CONTROL

`PROCEED WITH CONTROL` is not a lesser form of acceptance for an unresolved material problem.

It may only be used where:

- the relevant technical basis is sufficiently resolved;
- no material uncertainty remains unresolved;
- the identified control is documented;
- the control is capable of being implemented and verified;
- the condition remains within reasonable practitioner competence;
- no unresolved REFER, INVESTIGATE or ESCALATE action material to the decision remains.

`PROCEED WITH CONTROL` must not be used merely to avoid delay, referral, investigation, escalation or STOP.

---

## 9. Non-PROCEED Boundary

A positive CWIG gate decision is prohibited while any of the following remains material to the waterproofing decision:

- a CWI finding of **NOT ACCEPTABLE**;
- `UNRESOLVED — MATERIAL TO CWIG DECISION`;
- an unresolved INVESTIGATE action;
- an unresolved REFER action;
- an unresolved ESCALATE action;
- an identified waterproofing-system incompatibility;
- the waterproofing system has not been sufficiently identified;
- a required technical basis cannot be established;
- CWIG record status is `REASSESSMENT REQUIRED`.

Commercial pressure does not change this rule.

Builder instruction, owner instruction, project scheduling, following-trade availability or financial consequences cannot convert an unresolved material condition into:

**PROCEED**

or

**PROCEED WITH CONTROL**.

---

## 10. Decision States

CWIG gate decisions are:

- **PROCEED**
- **PROCEED WITH CONTROL**
- **INVESTIGATE**
- **REFER**
- **ESCALATE**
- **STOP**

These are separate from CWIG record-status controls:

- **VALID**
- **REASSESSMENT REQUIRED**
- **SUPERSEDED**

A record may therefore be:

**VALID — STOP**

if STOP is the current valid decision.

---

## 11. Change After Assessment

CWIG records the interface presented at a particular point in time.

A later material change may invalidate the basis of the earlier decision.

Potential changes include:

- substrate alteration;
- damage;
- new or altered penetration;
- drain/interface change;
- geometry change;
- waterstop/threshold change;
- waterproofing-system or component substitution;
- contamination;
- material moisture event;
- changed junction;
- contradictory technical information.

Where a material change occurs:

> **REASSESSMENT REQUIRED**

applies to the affected domain or domains before waterproofing proceeds.

A separate pre-application status reconfirmation control is used during the pilot immediately before membrane application.

---

## 12. Concealed and Unavailable Conditions

CWIG cannot establish facts that are not reasonably observable or supported by available evidence.

A positive CWIG decision does not prove that:

- concealed framing is compliant;
- concealed fixings are correct;
- hidden plumbing is compliant;
- hidden structural conditions are satisfactory;
- every earlier construction stage was correctly completed.

Missing or unavailable evidence must not improve the CWIG outcome.

---

## 13. Independent Review

Some pilot cases may receive independent comparison review.

Independent pilot review is used to test:

- assessor reproducibility;
- interpretation;
- technical-basis selection;
- decision consistency.

An independent pilot review does not convert CWIG into a statutory inspection or regulatory approval.

---

## 14. Pilot Status

CWIG-01 is currently an independently developed controlled pilot concept.

The pilot is intended to test whether the proposed interface gate provides useful and proportionate assurance.

It has not established:

- defect reduction;
- regulatory effectiveness;
- industry-wide suitability;
- mandatory implementation;
- statutory recognition.

A pilot outcome that shows CWIG should be narrowed, modified, paused or abandoned is legitimate evidence.

---

## 15. Required Use of This Statement

This document is the canonical CWIG-01 Scope & Limitation Statement for the controlled pilot.

Its core meaning should be incorporated into or provided with:

- CWIG-01 Pilot Record Form;
- future CWIG Pilot Participant Information & Consent Record (once issued);
- assessor information;
- builder/project-controller information;
- independent reviewer information;
- participant-facing pilot material.

No shorter summary should materially broaden what CWIG means.

No participant-facing document should imply a level of certification, authority, inspection or assurance greater than this statement provides.

---

## 16. Short Participant-Facing Statement

Where a concise notice is required, use:

> **CWIG-01 is a limited pre-waterproofing interface assessment used in a controlled pilot. It records whether the observable and reasonably measurable interface presented at the time is suitable for the identified waterproofing system to proceed. It is not a compliance certificate, statutory inspection, approval of upstream work or guarantee against future leakage. It does not transfer another practitioner's existing responsibilities and has no government or regulatory status.**

The full Scope & Limitation Statement remains controlling where any interpretation question arises.
