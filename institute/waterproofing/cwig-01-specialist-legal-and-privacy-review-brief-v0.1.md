# PART A — PILOT CONTEXT

## 3. What CWIG-01 Is

CWIG-01 means:

**Critical Waterproofing Interface Gate — CWIG-01**

It is a proposed controlled pre-waterproofing decision process.

Its purpose is to test whether observable and reasonably measurable waterproofing-interface conditions can be assessed and recorded more consistently before membrane installation.

CWIG-01 currently contains six domains:

1. **CWI-01 — Substrate Acceptance**
2. **CWI-02 — Water-Management Geometry**
3. **CWI-03 — Drainage and Penetration Interface**
4. **CWI-04 — Junction and Movement Treatability**
5. **CWI-05 — Surface and Moisture Acceptance**
6. **CWI-06 — Waterproofing System Compatibility**

---

## 4. What CWIG-01 Is Not

CWIG-01 is not intended to be:

- a statutory inspection;
- a building approval;
- waterproofing certification;
- plumbing certification;
- certification of framing or structural work;
- certification of upstream trade work;
- a government inspection;
- government endorsement;
- a guarantee against waterproofing failure.

It currently has no statutory status.

---

## 5. Initial Pilot Scope

The proposed initial cohort is small:

> **approximately 5–10 controlled Class 1 residential bathroom / ensuite / shower-area cases containing a shower**

The pilot is intended to test feasibility and governance.

It is not statistically powered to establish an industry-wide defect-reduction rate.

## 8. Existing Information-Governance Principles

The current architecture proposes:

- data minimisation;
- Pilot Case IDs rather than unnecessary names in research records;
- separation of participant-identifying information from cross-case evidence where practical;
- separate permission for controlled pilot photographs and external/public image use;
- append-only preservation of material adverse pilot findings;
- version-controlled correction rather than deletion of earlier technical findings;
- no invented retention period;
- no promise of automatic record deletion on withdrawal;
- specialist review before determining disclosure obligations;
- no automatic field use of the consent record.

The specialist is requested to determine which of these controls are legally appropriate and what must change.

# PART AD — REVIEW PRINCIPLE

## 70. Smallest Proportionate Legal Architecture

The requested specialist advice should not assume that maximum legal documentation is automatically better governance.

The desired outcome is:

> **the smallest legally sound, ethically defensible and operationally practical participant and information-governance architecture capable of supporting a credible controlled pilot.**

Where a simpler control provides equivalent protection:

identify it.

Where a proposed CWIG control creates unnecessary legal complexity:

identify it.

Where the pilot should not proceed:

state so clearly.

---

## 71. No Predetermined Outcome

The specialist is not being asked to approve CWIG.

The reviewer is expressly invited to conclude that:

- the proposed consent architecture is inadequate;
- another operating entity is required;
- data collection should be narrowed;
- photography should be restricted;
- ethics review is necessary;
- specific pilot activities should be removed;
- the pilot should remain HOLD.

Adverse specialist advice must be preserved in the governance record.
