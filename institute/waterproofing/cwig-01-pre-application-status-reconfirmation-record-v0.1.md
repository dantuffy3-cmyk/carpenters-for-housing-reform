# CWIG-01 — Pre-Application Status Reconfirmation Record v0.1

**Prepared by:** Carpenters for Housing Reform Victoria  
**Status:** Working Draft — Controlled Pre-Pilot Field Record v0.1  
**Date:** August 2026  
**Purpose:** Provide a short operational control immediately before waterproofing membrane application to determine whether a material change has occurred since the current CWIG-01 assessment and whether reassessment is required before waterproofing proceeds.

---

## 1. Purpose

CWIG-01 assesses the waterproofing interface at a defined point in time.

Conditions may change after that assessment and before membrane application.

Examples include:

- substrate alteration;
- new penetration;
- drain movement;
- changed waterstop or threshold;
- product substitution;
- contamination;
- moisture event;
- damage;
- work by another practitioner;
- contradictory technical information.

A previously valid CWIG record cannot react automatically to those changes.

This record therefore provides a short:

> **pre-application status reconfirmation**

immediately before waterproofing membrane application.

---

## 2. What This Record Is

This record asks:

> **Is the interface about to be waterproofed materially unchanged from the interface on which the current CWIG decision was made?**

It is a:

- change-control check;
- status-validity control;
- trigger for reassessment where required;
- controlled pilot evidence record.

---

## 3. What This Record Is Not

This record is not:

- a new CWIG assessment;
- a seventh CWI domain;
- a statutory inspection;
- a compliance certificate;
- a waterproofing certificate;
- certification of upstream work;
- certification of plumbing;
- certification of framing or lining;
- a building approval;
- government approval;
- a substitute for the CWIG-01 Pilot Record Form;
- proof that no concealed condition exists.

It does not expand the scope of CWIG-01.

---

## 4. Relationship to CWIG-01

The six CWIG domains remain:

1. **CWI-01 — Substrate Acceptance**
2. **CWI-02 — Water-Management Geometry**
3. **CWI-03 — Drainage and Penetration Interface**
4. **CWI-04 — Junction and Movement Treatability**
5. **CWI-05 — Surface and Moisture Acceptance**
6. **CWI-06 — Waterproofing System Compatibility**

This record does not create another domain.

Where material change is identified, the relevant existing CWI domain or domains are reassessed.

## 6. Preconditions

Before completing this record, confirm:

- [ ] a current CWIG assessment exists;
- [ ] the current CWIG assessment can be identified;
- [ ] the interface is sufficiently visible to identify material change;
- [ ] waterproofing membrane application has not yet commenced;
- [ ] the practitioner is about to commence or authorise commencement of waterproofing within the scope of the pilot.

If the current assessment cannot be identified:

> **REASSESSMENT REQUIRED**

until the basis for proceeding is established.

---

# PART B — RECONFIRMATION STATES

## 7. Permitted Reconfirmation States

For each item use one of:

### NO MATERIAL CHANGE IDENTIFIED

The presented condition appears materially consistent with the current CWIG assessment.

### MATERIAL CHANGE IDENTIFIED

A change has occurred that may affect the basis of the current CWIG decision.

### UNABLE TO CONFIRM

The practitioner cannot reasonably determine whether a material change has occurred.

### NOT APPLICABLE

The item does not apply to the project configuration.

---

## 8. Controlling Rule

> **A MATERIAL CHANGE IDENTIFIED or UNABLE TO CONFIRM result cannot improve the CWIG outcome.**

Where the change may affect the basis of the current CWIG decision:

> **CWIG RECORD STATUS = REASSESSMENT REQUIRED**

until the relevant affected domain or domains have been reassessed.

## 10. CWI-01 — Substrate Acceptance

### Question

- [ ] NO MATERIAL CHANGE IDENTIFIED

- sheet replacement;
- patching;
- damage;
- movement;
- loss of fixity;
- newly exposed defect;

## 18. Outcome A — No Material Change Identified

Select only where:

- no material change has been identified;
- no relevant item remains unable to be confirmed;
- the current CWIG record remains applicable;
- no material contradictory technical information has arisen.

### Outcome

- [ ] **NO MATERIAL CHANGE IDENTIFIED**

### CWIG Record Status

`VALID`

### Current Gate Decision

Record the existing gate decision:

- [ ] PROCEED
- [ ] PROCEED WITH CONTROL
- [ ] INVESTIGATE
- [ ] REFER
- [ ] ESCALATE
- [ ] STOP

### Critical Control

This reconfirmation does **not** upgrade the existing gate decision.

Example:

If the current record is:

`VALID — STOP`

and no material change has occurred:

the record remains:

`VALID — STOP`

It does not become PROCEED merely because reconfirmation was completed.

# PART F — TARGETED REASSESSMENT

## 21. Reassessment Should Be Proportionate

A material change does not automatically require all six domains to be reassessed.

Reassess the domain or domains materially affected.

Indicative examples:

| Change | Potentially affected domain(s) |
|---|---|
| substrate replacement | CWI-01, CWI-05, CWI-06 where relevant |
| changed shower geometry | CWI-02 |
| new penetration | CWI-03 and others if materially affected |
| changed junction | CWI-04 |
| contamination/moisture event | CWI-05 |
| waterproofing system substitution | CWI-06 and any dependent domain |
| changed waste | CWI-02 / CWI-03 as applicable |

This table is an operational guide only.

The actual affected domain depends on the project condition.

---

## 22. Reassessment Sequence

Where reassessment is required:

**MATERIAL CHANGE IDENTIFIED**

↓

**REASSESSMENT REQUIRED**

↓

**AFFECTED CWI DOMAIN(S) IDENTIFIED**

↓

**REASSESSMENT COMPLETED**

↓

**NEW EVIDENCE PRESERVED**

↓

**CURRENT GATE DECISION ISSUED**

↓

if the new assessment replaces the earlier assessment:

**EARLIER RECORD RETAINED / SUPERSEDED AS APPROPRIATE**

↓

**PRE-APPLICATION STATUS RECONFIRMATION COMPLETED AGAIN IF REQUIRED BEFORE MEMBRANE APPLICATION**

---

# PART G — ADVERSE EVIDENCE

## 23. Preservation Rule

A material change identified during reconfirmation is pilot evidence.

It must not be removed simply because it is corrected before waterproofing.

Preserve:

- original CWIG decision;
- reconfirmation event;
- material change;
- action taken;
- reassessment;
- final current decision.

---

## 24. Append-Only Principle

> **Once a material reconfirmation finding has been entered into the controlled pilot record, later correction creates a linked subsequent entry. The original finding is not overwritten or deleted.**

This protects the pilot from retrospective sanitisation.

## 28. Hard Gate

Before membrane application:

> **If the current CWIG record status is REASSESSMENT REQUIRED, waterproofing does not proceed under the earlier CWIG decision.**

Reassessment must first establish the current decision.

---

# PART K — PILOT GOVERNANCE

## 29. Pilot-Level Escalation

A reconfirmation event should be considered for pilot governance review where:

- waterproofing proceeds despite `REASSESSMENT REQUIRED`;
- a known material change is intentionally not recorded;
- commercial pressure materially affects the recorded decision;
- repeated material changes are not being notified;
- practitioners repeatedly cannot determine whether reconfirmation is required;
- evidence integrity is compromised.

A routine material change that is correctly identified, reassessed and resolved is not automatically a failure of CWIG.

It may demonstrate that the reconfirmation control is functioning as intended.

## 31. Canonical Boundary
