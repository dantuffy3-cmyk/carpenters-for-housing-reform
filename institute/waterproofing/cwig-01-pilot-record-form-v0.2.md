# CWIG-01 — Critical Waterproofing Interface Gate
## Pilot Record Form v0.2

**Prepared by:** Carpenters for Housing Reform Victoria  
**Status:** Working Draft — Controlled Pilot Field Record v0.2  
**Date:** August 2026  
**Purpose:** Practical field-testing record for the Critical Waterproofing Interface Gate (CWIG-01). This form incorporates the four refinements identified through the CWIG-01 Worked Pilot Simulation Sprint v0.1. It is not a statutory certificate, government-endorsed process, building approval, plumbing certificate or final regulatory mechanism.

---

## 1. Purpose of This Record

CWIG-01 tests whether a small number of observable or reasonably measurable conditions can be assessed immediately before waterproofing so that unsuitable interfaces are identified before membrane installation.

The controlling question is:

> **Has the waterproofing practitioner been presented with an interface on which the identified waterproofing system can responsibly proceed?**

CWIG-01 does not determine whether the entire bathroom is compliant.

It does not certify upstream trades.

It records the condition of the waterproofing interface presented at the time of assessment.

---

## 2. Initial Pilot Scope

CWIG-01 Pilot Record Form v0.2 is limited for initial controlled field testing to:

- Class 1 residential bathrooms;
- Class 1 residential ensuites;
- Class 1 residential shower areas.

The following remain outside the initial v0.2 pilot population:

- laundries without a shower;
- balconies;
- external waterproofing;
- below-ground waterproofing;
- Class 2–9 wet areas;
- unusual Performance Solutions unless separately reviewed;
- other wet-area configurations not yet calibrated for this form.

Exclusion from the initial pilot does not mean CWIG can never apply to those areas.

It means the first pilot population is deliberately narrow so that evidence can be interpreted consistently.

---

## 3. Gate Decision States

Use one of the following gate decision states wherever required.

### PROCEED

The relevant condition is acceptable and waterproofing may proceed.

### PROCEED WITH CONTROL

The condition can proceed only with a specific documented control.

The control must be recorded.

### INVESTIGATE

More evidence or investigation is required before a reliable decision can be made.

### REFER

Another appropriate practitioner or responsible party must resolve the condition.

### ESCALATE

Higher-order technical, design, manufacturer, engineering, regulatory or other competent advice is required.

### STOP

The current condition prevents reliable waterproofing from proceeding.

---

## 4. Evidence States

Use one or more evidence states for each material finding.

### OBSERVED

The practitioner directly observed the condition.

### MEASURED

A measurement was taken and recorded.

### PHOTO_SUPPORTED

A photograph supports what was visible at the relevant time.

A photograph does not automatically prove compliance.

### DOCUMENT_SUPPORTED

A drawing, specification, certificate, technical document or controlled project record supports the entry.

### MANUFACTURER_SUPPORTED

Relevant manufacturer installation guidance, technical data or documented system requirements support the decision.

### INDEPENDENTLY_VERIFIED

A separate competent person independently reviewed the condition.

### UNRESOLVED — MATERIAL TO CWIG DECISION

Information necessary to determine whether waterproofing can responsibly proceed has not been established.

This state cannot support a final **PROCEED** decision until the material issue is resolved.

### NOT VERIFIED — OUTSIDE CWIG SCOPE

The matter has not been certified or verified because it falls outside the defined CWIG practitioner acceptance boundary.

This state does not automatically prevent **PROCEED**, provided the unverified matter is not necessary to determine suitability of the observable or reasonably measurable waterproofing interface.

---

## 5. Uncertainty Control

CWIG v0.2 deliberately distinguishes between:

> **Something CWIG needs to know but cannot yet establish**

and:

> **Something CWIG is not intended to certify.**

The first is:

`UNRESOLVED — MATERIAL TO CWIG DECISION`

The second is:

`NOT VERIFIED — OUTSIDE CWIG SCOPE`

Missing information must never be converted into positive evidence.

Where a matter initially considered outside CWIG scope becomes necessary to determine waterproofing-interface suitability, it must be reclassified as material and handled through:

- INVESTIGATE;
- REFER;
- ESCALATE; or
- STOP.

## 11. Mandatory Reassessment Triggers

The following events require consideration of whether CWIG status becomes:

**REASSESSMENT REQUIRED**

### Waterproofing-System Changes

- waterproofing manufacturer changed;
- membrane product changed;
- waterproofing system changed;
- primer requirement changed;
- junction-treatment system changed;
- drainage-interface detail changed;
- significant manufacturer instruction changes become known.

### Substrate Changes

- substrate replaced;
- substrate materially repaired;
- substrate damaged;
- substrate becomes contaminated;
- substrate moisture condition materially changes;
- surface preparation is materially altered.

### Geometry Changes

- shower configuration changes;
- hob changes;
- threshold changes;
- set-down changes;
- waterstop changes;
- falls are altered;
- drainage geometry changes.

### Drainage and Penetration Changes

- waste replaced;
- waste moved;
- drainage interface changed;
- penetration added;
- penetration moved;
- penetration enlarged;
- plumbing interface materially altered.

### Junction and Movement Changes

- junction geometry changes;
- material transition changes;
- movement condition changes;
- niche or recess changes;
- interface requiring a different treatment is created.

### Other Material Changes

- damage occurs after CWIG assessment;
- another practitioner materially modifies the assessed interface;
- significant moisture event occurs;
- significant contamination event occurs;
- new evidence contradicts the original assessment;
- any other condition on which the original gate decision relied materially changes.

# PART I — PILOT GOVERNANCE

## 33. Interpretation Controls

This pilot record must not be interpreted as proving that:

- the entire bathroom is compliant;
- all upstream work is compliant;
- concealed construction has been verified;
- plumbing compliance has been certified;
- structural adequacy has been certified;
- a future waterproofing failure cannot occur;
- a STOP condition definitely would have caused a future defect;
- a photograph proves compliance;
- manufacturer documentation proves correct installation;
- a CWIG pass replaces PCR-01;
- a CWIG pass replaces appropriate building, plumbing, surveying, engineering or regulatory functions;
- a `NOT VERIFIED — OUTSIDE CWIG SCOPE` entry means the underlying work is compliant;
- a previously valid CWIG remains valid after material change without reassessment.

CWIG-01 tests one narrower proposition:

> **Whether unsuitable observable or reasonably measurable waterproofing-interface conditions can be identified and acted upon before membrane installation.**

# PART J — RELATIONSHIP TO THE WIDER ASSURANCE ARCHITECTURE

## 36. Assurance Sequence

CWIG-01 occurs before waterproofing installation.

The emerging sequence remains:

**UPSTREAM TRADE WORK**

↓

**UPSTREAM EVIDENCE WHERE JUSTIFIED**

↓

**CWIG-01 — CRITICAL WATERPROOFING INTERFACE GATE**

↓

**WATERPROOFING INSTALLATION**

↓

**PCR-01 — PRE-CONCEALMENT RELEASE**

↓

**TILING / FINISHES**

↓

**PMP-01 — POST-MEMBRANE PROTECTION CONTROL**

↓

**PERMANENT WATERPROOFING ASSURANCE RECORD**

CWIG-01 must not absorb the functions of PCR-01 or PMP-01.

---

## 37. Core Field Rule

> **If the practitioner cannot responsibly determine that the presented interface is suitable for the identified waterproofing system, waterproofing should not proceed merely because the project schedule expects it to.**

The appropriate decision is:

**INVESTIGATE**

**REFER**

**ESCALATE**

or

**STOP**

until the material issue is resolved.

A CWIG decision applies only to the materially unchanged interface and waterproofing system that were assessed.

## 38. v0.2 Change Control

CWIG-01 Pilot Record Form v0.2 introduces only the four refinements supported by the Worked Pilot Simulation Sprint v0.1.

### Refinement 1 — Technical-Detail Traceability

CWI-02 now requires:

**configuration → applicable technical detail → measurement → decision**

### Refinement 2 — Split Uncertainty

The previous broad uncertainty treatment is separated into:

`UNRESOLVED — MATERIAL TO CWIG DECISION`

and:

`NOT VERIFIED — OUTSIDE CWIG SCOPE`

### Refinement 3 — Gate Validity and Reassessment

CWIG now records:

- VALID;
- REASSESSMENT REQUIRED;
- SUPERSEDED.

Material changes trigger reassessment.

### Refinement 4 — Narrow Initial Pilot Scope

Initial controlled field testing is limited to:

**Class 1 residential bathrooms, ensuites and shower areas.**

No other substantive CWIG architecture is intentionally changed in v0.2.
