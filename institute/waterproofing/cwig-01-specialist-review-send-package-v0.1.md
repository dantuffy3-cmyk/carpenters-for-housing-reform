# PART A — PURPOSE OF THE PACKAGE

## 2. Why This Package Exists

CWIG-01 has reached the point where internal policy, technical and governance development can no longer appropriately resolve several legal and privacy questions.

Those questions include:

- which legal entity should operate the pilot;
- which privacy laws apply;
- what participant consent or acknowledgment is required;
- how residential photographs should be handled;
- what information may be retained;
- how withdrawal should work;
- what disclosure obligations may exist;
- what security architecture is proportionate;
- whether research-ethics review is required or advisable;
- what legal wording is required before the pilot may proceed.

The purpose of this package is to enable a suitably qualified external specialist to answer those questions without requiring the specialist to reconstruct the entire CWIG development history.

---

## 3. Core Specialist Question

> **Is the proposed CWIG-01 participant, privacy, information-handling and pilot-governance architecture legally and ethically defensible for a small controlled Victorian field pilot — and if not, what minimum changes are required before field participation can occur?**

---

## 4. Specialist Is Not Being Asked to Approve CWIG

The specialist is not being asked to determine:

- whether CWIG is technically superior;
- whether CWIG reduces waterproofing defects;
- whether CWIG should become mandatory;
- whether CWIG should become statutory;
- whether government should adopt CWIG;
- whether the six-domain architecture is technically correct;
- whether a particular bathroom should receive PROCEED or STOP.

Those matters remain outside this specialist review unless a legal issue directly depends on them.

# PART C — REVIEW CONTEXT

## 7. CWIG-01 in One Paragraph

CWIG-01 means:

**Critical Waterproofing Interface Gate — CWIG-01**

It is a proposed controlled pre-waterproofing decision process intended to test whether observable and reasonably measurable waterproofing-interface conditions can be assessed, documented and acted upon more consistently before membrane application.

CWIG is currently experimental and non-statutory.

---

## 8. Six CWIG Domains

The architecture contains six domains:

1. **CWI-01 — Substrate Acceptance**
2. **CWI-02 — Water-Management Geometry**
3. **CWI-03 — Drainage and Penetration Interface**
4. **CWI-04 — Junction and Movement Treatability**
5. **CWI-05 — Surface and Moisture Acceptance**
6. **CWI-06 — Waterproofing System Compatibility**

The specialist review is not intended to redesign these domains.

---

## 9. Initial Pilot Scale

The proposed initial pilot is approximately:

> **5–10 controlled Class 1 residential bathroom / ensuite / shower-area cases containing a shower**

The purpose is feasibility and governance testing.

The cohort is not intended to establish an industry-wide defect-reduction rate.

---

## 10. Current Governance State

The pilot architecture has already undergone:

- technical verification;
- adversarial simulation;
- assessor-calibration development;
- pilot-record refinement;
- independent pre-pilot review;
- independent-review reconciliation;
- scope and limitation control;
- pre-application change-control development;
- pre-pilot governance correction;
- participant-information drafting.

Despite this development:

> **no field participation is currently authorised.**
