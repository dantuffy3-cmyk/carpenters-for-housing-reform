# Pilot Observer Protocol — v0.1

**Status:** WORKING DRAFT

**Prepared by:** Carpenters for Housing Reform Victoria
**Version:** 0.1
**Date:** August 2026

---

## Important Notice

> "Observation of the pilot by a government, regulatory or industry representative does not constitute endorsement of the pilot, assessment framework, participant outcomes or any proposed statutory model."

This statement must be included in all observer briefing materials and observer agreements.

---

## Purpose

This protocol governs the terms under which observers may access and observe ARC capability-assessment pilot activities. It protects participant privacy, evidence integrity, assessor independence and the validity of the pilot.

---

## Who May Observe

Potential observers include representatives of:

- the Department of Transport and Planning (DTP);
- the Building and Plumbing Commission (BPC);
- industry bodies (carpentry, building, trade associations);
- registered training organisations;
- insurers or risk bodies;
- consumer organisations;
- other relevant regulatory or policy bodies.

Observer access is governed and not automatic. A request for observer access must be assessed by the pilot coordinator and approved by the pilot sponsor before access is granted.

---

## Observer Role

Observers may:

- observe governance processes and assessment activities to the extent permitted under their observer agreement;
- ask questions through the process defined in this protocol;
- receive approved documents and summaries;
- observe without directing or interfering.

Observers may not:

- access identifiable participant data without separate consent;
- direct, instruct or interfere with assessment activities;
- communicate with candidates about assessment content;
- represent their observation as endorsement;
- make governance or assessment decisions on behalf of the pilot.

---

## Access

Observer access is determined by the pilot sponsor on a case-by-case basis. Access may be:

- **Document access only:** Approved documents and de-identified summaries are provided.
- **Process observation:** Observer may attend governance or calibration meetings.
- **Assessment observation:** Observer may observe assessment activities where participants have separately consented.

Participant consent is required before any observer may observe an individual assessment session involving that participant.

---

## Confidentiality

All observers must sign a confidentiality agreement before receiving any pilot materials or accessing any pilot activity.

The confidentiality agreement must include:

- agreement not to disclose identifiable participant information;
- agreement not to disclose assessment scenario content;
- agreement that observation does not constitute endorsement;
- agreement to use pilot information only for permitted purposes;
- acknowledgment of the pilot's working draft status.

Confidentiality agreements are filed by the pilot coordinator and recorded in the Pilot Decision Register.

---

## Questions

Observers may ask questions about the pilot process through the following mechanism:

- Questions are submitted in writing to the pilot coordinator.
- Questions are reviewed and responses are prepared by the pilot coordinator or pilot sponsor.
- Responses are provided in writing.
- Questions and responses are recorded in the Pilot Decision Register.

Observers do not have the right to question individual participants during assessment activities.

---

## Conflict Declaration

All observers must complete a Conflict of Interest Declaration (`docs/pilot-conflict-of-interest-declaration-v0.1.md`) before access is granted.

An observer with an undisclosed conflict that is identified during the pilot may have their access suspended pending review.

---

## Non-Endorsement

The following applies to all observer participation:

1. Observation does not constitute endorsement of the pilot.
2. Observation does not constitute endorsement of the assessment framework.
3. Observation does not constitute endorsement of any participant outcome.
4. Observation does not constitute endorsement of any proposed statutory model.
5. Observers who are government or regulatory representatives do not acquire decision-making authority over the pilot merely by observing.
6. The pilot must not represent observer participation as government, regulatory or industry endorsement in any document or public communication.

---

## Recording Observer Participation

All observer access is recorded in the Pilot Decision Register, including:

- observer name and organisation;
- access level granted;
- dates of access;
- confidentiality agreement date;
- conflict declaration outcome;
- any questions raised and responses given.

---

## Observer Briefing

Before access is granted, observers receive:

- this Observer Protocol;
- the Candidate Information Statement;
- the Governance Plan summary;
- the non-endorsement statement;
- the confidentiality agreement.

---

## Withdrawal of Observer Access

Observer access may be withdrawn by the pilot sponsor at any time where:

- the observer breaches confidentiality;
- the observer interferes with pilot activities;
- the observer represents their observation as endorsement;
- a conflict is identified that was not declared;
- other governance concerns arise.

Withdrawal of observer access is recorded in the Pilot Decision Register.
