# Pilot Complaints and Review Process — v0.1

**Status:** WORKING DRAFT

**Prepared by:** Carpenters for Housing Reform Victoria
**Version:** 0.1
**Date:** August 2026

---

## Purpose

This process defines how complaints from pilot participants are received, recorded, reviewed and resolved. It also defines the separation between process review and re-assessment.

---

## Who May Raise a Complaint

Any pilot participant may raise a complaint, including:

- candidate carpenters;
- assessors;
- observers;
- consumer test participants;
- technical or governance reviewers.

---

## What Complaints May Relate To

| Category | Examples |
|---|---|
| Recruitment | Recruitment process not fairly described; channel bias; inadequate information |
| Eligibility | Eligibility criteria applied inconsistently; incorrect reason code assigned |
| Assessor conduct | Assessor bias; unprofessional behaviour; departure from framework |
| Conflict management | Undisclosed conflict; conflict not managed in accordance with declaration outcome |
| Assessment administration | Process not followed; scenario not administered correctly; recording issues |
| Privacy | Data not handled in accordance with the Privacy and Data Handling Plan |
| Communication | Inaccurate, misleading or delayed communication |
| Process fairness | General concern about fairness of process |
| Record accuracy | A record about the complainant contains an error |

A complaint must identify at least one of these categories.

---

## What Complaints Do Not Automatically Do

**Raising a complaint does not automatically change an assessment result.**

Process review and re-assessment are separate processes. A successful process complaint may lead to a re-assessment being considered, but only through the Re-Assessment Review process defined below.

---

## How to Raise a Complaint

Complaints are raised in writing (email or written form) to the pilot coordinator.

The complaint must include:

- the complainant's Participant ID or name;
- the complaint category;
- a description of the concern;
- the outcome the complainant is seeking.

---

## Complaint Intake and Acknowledgment

1. The pilot coordinator records the complaint in the Complaint Log.
2. The complainant receives acknowledgment within a reasonable timeframe (to be defined before live pilot commencement).
3. A complaint reference number is assigned.

---

## Process Review

Process complaints are reviewed by a person who was not directly involved in the decision or activity complained about.

Where the complaint concerns the pilot coordinator, the review is escalated to the pilot sponsor or an independent reviewer.

The process reviewer:

- reviews the complaint and relevant records;
- may interview the complainant and other relevant persons;
- provides a written finding;
- records the finding in the Pilot Decision Register.

Possible process review findings:

| Finding | Meaning |
|---|---|
| PROCESS_FOLLOWED | The process was correctly followed. No change required. |
| PROCESS_ERROR_NO_IMPACT | A process error occurred but did not affect the relevant outcome. Remediation of records only. |
| PROCESS_ERROR_MATERIAL | A process error occurred that may have affected the outcome. See Re-Assessment Review. |
| UNABLE_TO_DETERMINE | Insufficient evidence to determine whether the process was correctly followed. |

---

## Re-Assessment Review

A re-assessment review is a separate process from a process complaint. It is triggered only when:

- a process review finds a PROCESS_ERROR_MATERIAL outcome; and
- the error affected an assessment activity.

A re-assessment review does not automatically change an assessment result. It results in one of:

| Finding | Meaning |
|---|---|
| NO_CHANGE | The error did not materially affect the assessment. Result stands. |
| SUPPLEMENTARY_ASSESSMENT | Additional assessment activity is required. |
| FULL_RE_ASSESSMENT | The assessment must be conducted again. |
| ASSESSMENT_VOIDED | The assessment cannot be relied upon and must be removed from the evidence set. |

Re-assessment review decisions are recorded in the Pilot Decision Register.

---

## Independent Review

Where a complaint cannot be resolved internally, or where the complainant disputes the process review finding, the complaint may be referred to an independent reviewer.

The mechanism for appointing an independent reviewer is an unresolved question. See Unresolved Question [Q-025].

---

## Escalation

| Situation | Escalation |
|---|---|
| Complaint about pilot coordinator | Escalate to pilot sponsor |
| Complaint about pilot sponsor | Escalate to independent reviewer |
| Complaint raises safety concern | Trigger Safety and Incident Protocol |
| Complaint raises privacy concern | Trigger Privacy and Data Handling Plan incident process |
| Complaint cannot be resolved internally | Refer to independent reviewer |
