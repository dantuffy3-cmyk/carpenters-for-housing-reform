# Advanced Residential Carpentry Pilot — Governance and Recruitment One-Page Summary

**Status:** WORKING DRAFT — for government and regulator review

**Prepared by:** Carpenters for Housing Reform Victoria
**Version:** 0.1
**Date:** August 2026

---

## What This Pilot Is

The Advanced Residential Carpentry (ARC) pilot tests whether a structured capability-assessment framework can reliably evaluate advanced residential renovation capability in carpenters.

**The pilot tests capability-assessment validity under controlled governance, not to grant recognition to selected participants.**

---

## What No Participant Receives

Participation in the pilot does not confer:

- registration;
- licence;
- builder authority;
- engineering authority;
- permit authority;
- surveyor authority;
- licensed-trade authority;
- contracting rights;
- government endorsement.

> "A pilot assessment outcome is evidence generated for the pilot only. It does not create or alter any statutory authority, registration, licence or contracting entitlement."

---

## Who Participates

| Category | Role |
|---|---|
| Candidate Carpenters | Undergo the capability assessment |
| Assessors | Apply the assessment framework under calibrated, governed conditions |
| Technical / Governance Reviewers | Provide specialist review of methodology and governance |
| Consumer Participants (where used) | Test role comprehension and communication clarity only |
| Observers | Observe governance and assessment processes (observation ≠ endorsement) |

---

## How Candidates Are Recruited

- Recruitment uses multiple channels: direct invitation, association circulation, public expression of interest, training provider referral, and professional network outreach.
- Recruitment does not rely solely on the founder's personal network.
- Recruitment does not favour known supporters of the reform.
- Candidates are selected to support useful variation in experience, project type and employment context — not to maximise pass rates.

---

## How Assessors Are Governed

- Assessors complete calibration before conducting assessments.
- Assessors declare conflicts before each candidate assignment.
- Assessors may not assess candidates with undisclosed prior relationships.
- Second assessors provide independent review.
- A calibration chair manages consistency and moderation.
- Assessor composition is not predetermined; it requires a separate governance decision.

---

## How Consent and Privacy Work

- Participation is voluntary.
- Consent is obtained before any assessment activity begins.
- Consent to participate is separate from consent to publication.
- Participant data uses Participant IDs to limit distribution of identifiable information.
- Aggregate pilot reports are anonymised unless separate publication consent is given.
- Privacy and retention obligations require legal and governance review before live pilot commencement.

---

## How Conflicts Are Managed

- All assessors, reviewers and governance participants must declare conflicts before commencing their role.
- Declared conflicts result in: no action, managed controls, or recusal.
- Undisclosed conflicts discovered during the pilot are treated as incidents.
- The pilot founder is not automatically assigned assessor authority.

---

## How Participant Bias Is Controlled

| Bias Risk | Control |
|---|---|
| Reform supporters over-recruited | Open EOI channel; no screening for reform position |
| Founder's network over-relied upon | At least one arm's-length channel required |
| Only experienced candidates selected | Experience variation actively sought |
| Only candidates expected to pass | Selection does not screen for expected performance |
| Geographic concentration | Diverse geographic distribution sought |
| Self-selection bias | Acknowledged in reporting |

The pilot does not claim statistical representativeness unless the design and sample support that claim.

---

## How the Pilot Can Pause or Stop

The pilot may be paused, narrowed, redesigned or stopped where:

- safety concerns arise;
- legal or regulatory boundary issues emerge;
- assessor divergence cannot be resolved;
- privacy or consent failures occur;
- evidence integrity is compromised;
- participant selection bias cannot be remedied;
- a government or regulatory body formally requests a pause where involved.

Pause and stop authority rests with the pilot sponsor. All pause and stop decisions are recorded in the Pilot Decision Register.

---

## Readiness Gate

No recruitment of actual candidates commences until a readiness checklist has been completed and a readiness outcome of `READY_TO_RECRUIT_LIMITED` or `READY_WITH_CONDITIONS` has been documented.

**This sprint does not commence live recruitment.**
