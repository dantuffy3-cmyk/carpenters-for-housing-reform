## Purpose

This template records the details of a Responsibility Handoff — an event that occurs when an Advanced Residential Carpenter identifies a condition requiring escalation, referral or work stoppage, and transfers a question, scope or responsibility to another practitioner or authority.

The record creates a traceable, governed account of:

- what was found;
- what decision was made;
- what work is paused or stopped;
- what was communicated to the consumer;
- what the receiving practitioner was asked to do;
- what conditions must be met before work resumes; and
- the final resolution state.

A Responsibility Handoff Record should be completed for every `ESCALATE`, `REFER` or `STOP` decision where:

- another practitioner is being engaged or consulted;
- consumer communication about a material issue is required; or
- work in the relevant area is paused pending resolution.

One record per distinct handoff. Multiple records may be opened for a single project.

---

## Controlled Handoff Status Values

Use only the following status classifications:

| Status | Meaning |
|---|---|
| `OPEN` | Handoff initiated; receiving practitioner not yet engaged |
| `AWAITING_INFORMATION` | Waiting for evidence, documents or access before referral can be issued |
| `REFERRED` | Question has been formally referred to the receiving practitioner |
| `ACCEPTED_BY_RECEIVING_PRACTITIONER` | Receiving practitioner has confirmed their involvement and scope |
| `CONDITIONAL` | Receiving practitioner has accepted but with conditions or limitations on scope |
| `RESOLVED` | The question has been resolved and work can resume or scope has been adjusted |
| `CLOSED_NO_ACTION` | Handoff closed without further action (e.g., investigation found no issue) |
| `UNRESOLVED` | The question has not been resolved; work in the affected area remains paused |
| `WORK_STOPPED` | Work is stopped and will not resume under current project conditions |

*Proportionate Escalation Test (record answers for each applicable question):*

1. What condition is known?
2. What is unknown?
3. What evidence supports the current understanding?
4. Is the condition within the practitioner's demonstrated capability?

6. Is another professional's judgement required?
7. Can the risk be controlled without changing responsibility?
8. Would continuing create unacceptable safety, consumer or regulatory risk?
9. Who must own the next decision?
10. What evidence is required before work resumes?

## Notes on Use

### Authority

This record does not transfer legal liability. Liability follows statutory responsibility under applicable Victorian law. The record documents governance decisions and practitioner reasoning.

### Consumer acknowledgement

Consumer acknowledgement records that the consumer was notified. It is not consent to a legal arrangement, a waiver of rights or an approval of a technical outcome. Where consumer consent has a legal dimension, `REQUIRES_LEGAL_OR_REGULATORY_REVIEW` applies.

### Multiple handoffs

A project may generate more than one Responsibility Handoff Record. Each record addresses a distinct condition or escalation event. Records should be cross-referenced using Handoff IDs.

### Pilot use

In the governed pilot, completed Responsibility Handoff Records form part of the evidence base for assessing candidate professional judgement. Assessors will examine:

- whether the decision state is appropriate to the condition;
- whether the proportionate escalation test was applied;
- whether the question referred to the receiving practitioner is precise;
- whether consumer communication is adequate; and
- whether resumption conditions are specific and measurable.

### Assessor calibration note

A well-completed Responsibility Handoff Record may demonstrate higher competence than a record that is absent because the practitioner proceeded without appropriate assessment. The decision to stop, refer or escalate — when correct — is evidence of capability.
