# Advanced Residential Carpentry Escalation Decision Matrix v0.1

**Carpenters for Housing Reform Victoria**
Version: 0.1
Date: August 2026
Status: WORKING DRAFT

> **Not legal advice.** This matrix is a governance and competency-assessment instrument. It does not determine legal liability, statutory registration scope, permit requirements, engineering necessity, builder responsibility, insurance coverage or compliance outcomes. Where law has not been verified, this document uses `REQUIRES_LEGAL_OR_REGULATORY_REVIEW`. This document does not imply government endorsement.

---

## Purpose

This matrix provides practical escalation scenarios for use in candidate assessment, assessor calibration and pilot governance. Each scenario illustrates conditions that may be encountered in residential renovation carpentry work and applies the escalation decision states and trigger categories defined in the Scope Escalation & Responsibility Resolution Framework v0.1.

**Decision states used in this matrix:**

- `PROCEED` — within capability and authority; no controls required
- `PROCEED_WITH_CONTROL` — may continue with documented controls
- `INVESTIGATE` — more evidence required before decision
- `REFER` — specific technical input required from another practitioner
- `ESCALATE` — operational decision ownership must move to another practitioner or authority; does not determine legal liability transfer
- `STOP` — work must not continue until material issue is resolved

**Evidence statuses used in this matrix:**

`USER_DECLARED` · `PHOTO_SUPPORTED` · `DOCUMENT_SUPPORTED` · `EXTERNAL_SOURCE` · `SYSTEM_DERIVED` · `PROFESSIONAL_REVIEWED` · `UNKNOWN` · `UNABLE_TO_VERIFY`

---

## How to Read This Matrix

Each row represents one scenario. Columns are:

| Column | Description |
|---|---|
| Trigger ID | Unique reference for this scenario |
| Scenario / Condition | Description of the discovered or encountered condition |
| Evidence Status | Classification of available information |
| Primary Risk | The principal risk if work proceeds without appropriate decision |
| Capability Question | Whether the practitioner's capability is adequate for this condition |
| Authority Question | Whether the practitioner holds lawful authority over the relevant scope |
| Initial Decision State | The governed decision state for this scenario |
| Potential Referral | Type of practitioner who may need to provide technical input |
| Potential Escalation | Type of practitioner or authority to whom operational decision ownership may need to move |
| Consumer Communication Required | Whether formal consumer notification is required |
| Work Allowed to Continue? | Whether other work may proceed while this condition is unresolved |
| Resumption Condition | What must occur before the paused scope resumes |
| Unresolved Issue | Legal, regulatory or authority question that remains open |
| Notes | Additional context, proportionality guidance or assessment notes |

## Escalation Scenarios

### Scenario ESC-001 — Severe Termite Damage to Floor Joists

| Field | Content |
|---|---|
| **Trigger ID** | ESC-001 |
| **Scenario / Condition** | During subfloor work, floor joists are found with severe termite damage. Extent is not yet fully visible. |
| **Evidence Status** | `PHOTO_SUPPORTED` (visible damage recorded); extent `UNKNOWN` |
| **Primary Risk** | Structural inadequacy of floor system; risk to persons if load-bearing capacity is compromised. |
| **Capability Question** | An Advanced Residential Carpenter may be capable of identifying termite damage and recognising its potential structural significance. Assessment of structural adequacy is a separate matter. |
| **Authority Question** | Assessment of structural adequacy and any required structural response may require engineering input. `REQUIRES_LEGAL_OR_REGULATORY_REVIEW` applies to the question of when engineering involvement is mandatory. |
| **Initial Decision State** | `STOP` (affected area); `INVESTIGATE` to determine extent |
| **Potential Referral** | Structural engineer, licensed pest inspector |
| **Potential Escalation** | Registered engineer (if structural adequacy is in question); registered builder (if scope of remediation is broader than a bounded carpentry scope) |
| **Consumer Communication Required** | Yes — consumer must be informed of the discovery, the paused scope and the specialist input required |
| **Work Allowed to Continue?** | Work in other areas unaffected by the structural risk may continue |
| **Resumption Condition** | Extent of damage assessed; structural adequacy confirmed or engineered remediation designed; consumer informed of revised scope |
| **Unresolved Issue** | Whether engineering sign-off is required for the specific remediation depends on scope and condition. `REQUIRES_LEGAL_OR_REGULATORY_REVIEW` |
| **Notes** | Termite damage in concealed areas is a progressive-discovery scenario. Its presence does not indicate practitioner error. Correct identification and escalation may demonstrate advanced capability. |

### Scenario ESC-002 — Unexpected Structural Framing Exposed During Bathroom Demolition

| Field | Content |
|---|---|
| **Trigger ID** | ESC-002 |
| **Scenario / Condition** | Bathroom demolition exposes structural framing that was not shown on any available documentation. The framing configuration is unexpected and its load-path function is unclear. |
| **Evidence Status** | `PHOTO_SUPPORTED` (condition recorded); `UNKNOWN` (load-path status); `UNABLE_TO_VERIFY` (original construction) |
| **Primary Risk** | Inadvertent removal or alteration of load-bearing construction; potential structural failure. |
| **Capability Question** | An Advanced Residential Carpenter may be capable of recognising unexpected framing and identifying that its function requires specialist assessment. |
| **Authority Question** | Load-path assessment and determination of whether the framing is load-bearing requires engineering input where reasonable doubt exists. |
| **Initial Decision State** | `STOP` (demolition in affected zone); `REFER` or `ESCALATE` to structural engineer |
| **Potential Referral** | Structural engineer |
| **Potential Escalation** | Structural engineer (for assessment); registered builder (if broader project coordination is affected) |
| **Consumer Communication Required** | Yes — consumer must be advised that demolition is paused pending structural assessment |
| **Work Allowed to Continue?** | Work in unaffected areas may continue. The demolished zone must not be altered further until assessment is received. |
| **Resumption Condition** | Structural engineer confirms load-path status and advises on safe approach; revised scope and sequencing documented |
| **Unresolved Issue** | Permit obligations may be affected if structural work is confirmed. `REQUIRES_LEGAL_OR_REGULATORY_REVIEW` |
| **Notes** | Unexpected framing is a common progressive-discovery scenario. The fact that documentation was not available is not itself a practitioner error. Stopping at this point demonstrates boundary recognition. |

### Scenario ESC-003 — Suspected Load-Bearing Wall Proposed for Removal

| Field | Content |
|---|---|
| **Trigger ID** | ESC-003 |
| **Scenario / Condition** | Consumer has instructed removal of a wall. Documentation is unavailable or inconclusive regarding whether the wall is load-bearing. |
| **Evidence Status** | `USER_DECLARED` (consumer's belief that it is not load-bearing); `UNABLE_TO_VERIFY` (structural function); `DOCUMENT_SUPPORTED` if partial documentation exists |
| **Primary Risk** | Removal of load-bearing construction without adequate temporary support or engineering design, causing structural failure. |
| **Capability Question** | An Advanced Residential Carpenter may be capable of assessing observable indicators of load-bearing function. Where doubt remains, this is insufficient for a proceed decision. |
| **Authority Question** | Engineering design for structural alteration or removal is not within the practitioner's authority. |
| **Initial Decision State** | `INVESTIGATE` (assess observable indicators); if doubt remains, `REFER` to structural engineer before work commences |
| **Potential Referral** | Structural engineer |
| **Potential Escalation** | Structural engineer; registered building surveyor (permit obligations may apply) |
| **Consumer Communication Required** | Yes — consumer must be advised that engineering input is required before wall removal can proceed |
| **Work Allowed to Continue?** | Other agreed work may continue. Wall removal must not commence until structural status is confirmed. |
| **Resumption Condition** | Structural assessment received; engineering design produced where required; permit obtained if required |
| **Unresolved Issue** | Permit requirement for structural alteration depends on scope and classification. `REQUIRES_LEGAL_OR_REGULATORY_REVIEW` |
| **Notes** | Consumer instruction that a wall is not load-bearing is `USER_DECLARED` only. It does not constitute verified evidence. The practitioner must not proceed on the basis of consumer assertion alone where observable indicators are ambiguous. |

### Scenario ESC-004 — Hidden Water Ingress Source Cannot Be Identified

| Field | Content |
|---|---|
| **Trigger ID** | ESC-004 |
| **Scenario / Condition** | During renovation work, water damage is found in concealed areas. The source of ingress cannot be identified from accessible areas. |
| **Evidence Status** | `PHOTO_SUPPORTED` (damage recorded); ingress source `UNKNOWN` |
| **Primary Risk** | Concealment of active water damage; inadequate remediation; further deterioration or mould development. |
| **Capability Question** | Diagnosis of concealed water ingress source may exceed carpentry capability where specialist investigation (e.g., thermal imaging, plumbing inspection) is required. |
| **Authority Question** | If the ingress involves plumbing systems, licensed-trade authority applies. |
| **Initial Decision State** | `INVESTIGATE` — specialist assessment of ingress source required |
| **Potential Referral** | Licensed plumber; building inspector; waterproofing specialist |
| **Potential Escalation** | Licensed plumber (if source is a plumbing system) |
| **Consumer Communication Required** | Yes — consumer must be advised of the discovery and the investigation required |
| **Work Allowed to Continue?** | Work in affected areas should pause. Unaffected areas may continue. |
| **Resumption Condition** | Ingress source identified; remediation plan agreed; licensed trade engaged where required |
| **Unresolved Issue** | Waterproofing obligations and licensed-trade requirements depend on scope and classification. `REQUIRES_LEGAL_OR_REGULATORY_REVIEW` |
| **Notes** | Proceeding without identifying the source risks enclosing active water damage. Investigation is required before remediation or reinstatement work. |

### Scenario ESC-005 — Subfloor Is Inaccessible

| Field | Content |
|---|---|
| **Trigger ID** | ESC-005 |
| **Scenario / Condition** | Subfloor access is required to assess floor structure, but the subfloor is inaccessible due to insufficient clearance or sealed openings. |
| **Evidence Status** | `UNABLE_TO_VERIFY` (subfloor condition) |
| **Primary Risk** | Proceeding without knowledge of subfloor condition; risk of undetected deterioration, pest damage or inadequate structure. |
| **Capability Question** | The practitioner cannot assess what cannot be observed. Inaccessibility is not evidence that conditions are satisfactory. |
| **Authority Question** | No authority question is triggered by inaccessibility per se. Authority questions arise from what is subsequently found. |
| **Initial Decision State** | `INVESTIGATE` — options to create access should be assessed and discussed with the consumer |
| **Potential Referral** | Building inspector or specialist as needed depending on what access reveals |
| **Potential Escalation** | Depends on findings once access is available |
| **Consumer Communication Required** | Yes — consumer must be advised that scope cannot be reliably confirmed without subfloor access |
| **Work Allowed to Continue?** | Work in areas not dependent on subfloor assessment may continue |
| **Resumption Condition** | Access created; subfloor condition assessed; scope revised if required |
| **Unresolved Issue** | None identified at this stage |
| **Notes** | `UNABLE_TO_VERIFY` cannot be treated as confirmation that conditions are satisfactory. Creating access may carry its own cost and scope implications that should be communicated to the consumer. |

### Scenario ESC-006 — Asbestos Suspected Behind Cladding

| Field | Content |
|---|---|
| **Trigger ID** | ESC-006 |
| **Scenario / Condition** | Removal of external or internal cladding is planned. Material suspected to be asbestos-containing is encountered. |
| **Evidence Status** | `PHOTO_SUPPORTED` (material recorded); `UNABLE_TO_VERIFY` (asbestos content without testing) |
| **Primary Risk** | Disturbance of asbestos-containing material without appropriate controls; risk to practitioner, consumer and others. |
| **Capability Question** | Identification and removal of asbestos-containing material requires specialist authority. Suspicion based on visual observation is reasonable; it is not diagnosis. |
| **Authority Question** | Asbestos removal is subject to Victorian regulatory requirements that limit who may perform removal. `REQUIRES_LEGAL_OR_REGULATORY_REVIEW` applies to the specific scope and threshold. |
| **Initial Decision State** | `STOP` (affected area) — work must cease in the area until asbestos testing and appropriate management are arranged |
| **Potential Referral** | Licensed asbestos assessor or hygienist |
| **Potential Escalation** | Licensed asbestos removalist (where removal is required) |
| **Consumer Communication Required** | Yes — immediately; consumer must be advised of the suspected material and that the area is stopped pending assessment |
| **Work Allowed to Continue?** | Work in unaffected areas may continue. No disturbance of suspected material may occur. |
| **Resumption Condition** | Testing confirms presence or absence of asbestos; management or removal plan in place and compliant with applicable regulation |
| **Unresolved Issue** | Specific regulatory thresholds for asbestos removal licensing and permit requirements. `REQUIRES_LEGAL_OR_REGULATORY_REVIEW` |
| **Notes** | Visual inspection cannot confirm or exclude asbestos-containing material. Stopping is the correct response to a credible suspicion. This is a safety obligation, not a conservative preference. |

### Scenario ESC-007 — Existing Unapproved Alteration Discovered

| Field | Content |
|---|---|
| **Trigger ID** | ESC-007 |
| **Scenario / Condition** | Work exposes a structural or building alteration that appears to have been completed without a permit and that may not comply with the Building Code of Australia or applicable standards. |
| **Evidence Status** | `PHOTO_SUPPORTED` (condition recorded); approval status `UNABLE_TO_VERIFY` |
| **Primary Risk** | Proceeding with work that relies on or integrates an unapproved and potentially non-compliant structure; potential permit and liability exposure. |
| **Capability Question** | Identification of a suspected unapproved alteration is within capability. Determination of compliance or regulatory consequence is not. |
| **Authority Question** | Building surveyor / permit authority must determine how the unapproved alteration is addressed. `REQUIRES_LEGAL_OR_REGULATORY_REVIEW` |
| **Initial Decision State** | `STOP` (affected area); `REFER` to registered building surveyor |
| **Potential Referral** | Registered building surveyor; relevant building authority |
| **Potential Escalation** | Registered building surveyor (for regulatory determination); registered builder (if the remediation scope is domestic building work) |
| **Consumer Communication Required** | Yes — consumer must be advised of the discovery and the regulatory referral required |
| **Work Allowed to Continue?** | Work dependent on the unapproved structure must not continue. Other work may proceed where it is genuinely independent. |
| **Resumption Condition** | Regulatory position clarified; building surveyor advises on appropriate path; any required permits or approvals in place |
| **Unresolved Issue** | Regulatory obligations for discovered unapproved work and the mechanism for retrospective assessment or remediation. `REQUIRES_LEGAL_OR_REGULATORY_REVIEW` |
| **Notes** | Discovery of an unapproved alteration does not automatically establish who is at fault or what remedy is required. The practitioner's role is to identify, stop and refer — not to determine regulatory consequence. |

### Scenario ESC-008 — Consumer Asks Practitioner to Coordinate Broader Project

| Field | Content |
|---|---|
| **Trigger ID** | ESC-008 |
| **Scenario / Condition** | During or after a bounded carpentry engagement, the consumer asks the practitioner to take on coordination, contracting or management responsibility for a broader range of renovation work involving multiple trades or scopes. |
| **Evidence Status** | `USER_DECLARED` (consumer instruction) |
| **Primary Risk** | Assumption of contracting, coordination or management responsibility beyond the practitioner's authority and potentially requiring builder registration under Victorian law. |
| **Capability Question** | Coordination capability is a recognised domain. This question is primarily one of authority, not capability alone. |
| **Authority Question** | Domestic building contracting and project management responsibilities are regulated under Victorian law. Whether this request requires builder registration depends on the scope and contracting arrangement. `REQUIRES_LEGAL_OR_REGULATORY_REVIEW` |
| **Initial Decision State** | `ESCALATE` — the question of whether this expanded role is lawful must be resolved before acceptance |
| **Potential Referral** | Legal or regulatory advice; BPC for guidance on scope thresholds |
| **Potential Escalation** | Registered builder (if the scope requires registered builder involvement) |
| **Consumer Communication Required** | Yes — consumer must be advised of the authority question and of the need to clarify the legal position before any expanded role is accepted |
| **Work Allowed to Continue?** | Original agreed carpentry scope may continue. The expanded coordination role must not be assumed until legal position is clarified. |
| **Resumption Condition** | Legal or regulatory position confirmed; appropriate contracting and responsibility structure in place |
| **Unresolved Issue** | Threshold at which coordination becomes domestic building work requiring registration. `REQUIRES_LEGAL_OR_REGULATORY_REVIEW` |
| **Notes** | Consumer instruction to take on a broader role cannot itself create authority the practitioner does not possess. This scenario is a direct test of boundary recognition as a positive competence domain. |

### Scenario ESC-009 — Licensed Trade Work Intersects Carpentry Scope

| Field | Content |
|---|---|
| **Trigger ID** | ESC-009 |
| **Scenario / Condition** | Planned carpentry work (e.g., bathroom renovation, kitchen fit-out, floor replacement) requires or intersects with plumbing or electrical work that must be performed or certified by a licensed tradesperson. |
| **Evidence Status** | `USER_DECLARED` (scope as described); extent of licensed-trade involvement `UNKNOWN` until full assessment |
| **Primary Risk** | Performance of restricted work without a licence; failure to obtain required licensed-trade certification; integration errors where trades have not coordinated. |
| **Capability Question** | Identification of where licensed-trade work is required is within capability. Performance of licensed-trade work is not. |
| **Authority Question** | Licensed-trade authority is defined by Victorian law. The practitioner does not hold this authority. |
| **Initial Decision State** | `REFER` — licensed trade must be engaged for the relevant scope; carpentry work may proceed where it does not require or pre-empt licensed-trade work |
| **Potential Referral** | Licensed plumber, licensed electrician, licensed waterproofer as applicable |
| **Potential Escalation** | Registered builder (if the intersection creates coordination complexity beyond a bounded trade scope) |
| **Consumer Communication Required** | Yes — consumer must understand which elements require licensed trades and what sequencing is required |
| **Work Allowed to Continue?** | Carpentry work unaffected by the licensed-trade scope may continue. Work that pre-empts, depends on or conflicts with licensed-trade work should be sequenced to avoid creating problems for the licensed trade. |
| **Resumption Condition** | Licensed trade engaged and scope coordinated; relevant work completed and certified by licensed trade before dependent carpentry work proceeds |
| **Unresolved Issue** | Specific certification and inspection requirements vary by trade and scope. `REQUIRES_LEGAL_OR_REGULATORY_REVIEW` for specific obligations. |
| **Notes** | Sequencing and coordination with licensed trades is an Advanced Residential Carpentry competency. The boundary is understanding where carpentry ends and licensed-trade work begins, and arranging accordingly. |

### Scenario ESC-010 — Engineer Detail Conflicts with Site Condition

| Field | Content |
|---|---|
| **Trigger ID** | ESC-010 |
| **Scenario / Condition** | An engineering specification or detail has been issued for the project, but site conditions encountered during work differ materially from what the detail assumed. The detail cannot be implemented as drawn. |
| **Evidence Status** | `DOCUMENT_SUPPORTED` (engineering detail); site condition `PHOTO_SUPPORTED`; adequacy of departure `UNABLE_TO_VERIFY` |
| **Primary Risk** | Implementation of an engineering detail in a manner that is materially different from what was assessed and specified, potentially compromising structural adequacy. |
| **Capability Question** | Identification that a conflict exists is within capability. Resolution of the conflict requires the engineer's judgement. |
| **Authority Question** | Only the engineer who issued the detail (or an equivalent qualified substitute) can revise or endorse a departure. |
| **Initial Decision State** | `STOP` (affected element); `REFER` to the issuing engineer |
| **Potential Referral** | Issuing structural engineer |
| **Potential Escalation** | Issuing structural engineer; registered building surveyor (if permit documentation is affected) |
| **Consumer Communication Required** | Yes — consumer must be advised that work is paused pending engineering review of the site condition |
| **Work Allowed to Continue?** | Work on other elements unaffected by the conflict may continue |
| **Resumption Condition** | Engineer reviews site condition and issues a revised or endorsed solution; revised documentation provided; any permit updates completed |
| **Unresolved Issue** | Permit documentation update obligations depend on scope of departure. `REQUIRES_LEGAL_OR_REGULATORY_REVIEW` |
| **Notes** | Implementation of a detail known to conflict with site conditions — without engineering review — transfers risk to the practitioner and may compromise structural adequacy. Stopping and referring is the correct response. |

### Scenario ESC-012 — Temporary Support Required Before Further Demolition

| Field | Content |
|---|---|
| **Trigger ID** | ESC-012 |
| **Scenario / Condition** | Planned demolition or removal will affect potentially load-bearing construction. Temporary propping or support is required before the work can safely proceed. |
| **Evidence Status** | `PHOTO_SUPPORTED` (construction visible); propping adequacy `UNABLE_TO_VERIFY` without engineering input |
| **Primary Risk** | Structural failure or instability if load-bearing elements are removed without adequate temporary support. |
| **Capability Question** | Understanding of temporary support principles is a recognised capability domain. Whether a practitioner can design and specify adequate propping without engineering input depends on the specific condition and scope. |
| **Authority Question** | Where engineering design for temporary support is required, engineering authority applies. `REQUIRES_LEGAL_OR_REGULATORY_REVIEW` |
| **Initial Decision State** | `INVESTIGATE` — assess whether the temporary support requirement can be managed within practitioner capability, or whether engineering input is required |
| **Potential Referral** | Structural engineer (if engineering design for propping is required) |
| **Potential Escalation** | Structural engineer (if engineering design is required); registered building surveyor (if permit obligations apply) |
| **Consumer Communication Required** | Yes — consumer must be advised that demolition is contingent on adequate temporary support being in place |
| **Work Allowed to Continue?** | Demolition of load-bearing elements must not proceed without adequate temporary support confirmed |
| **Resumption Condition** | Temporary support designed and installed; independent confirmation obtained where engineering input was required |
| **Unresolved Issue** | Threshold for when engineering input is required for temporary works. `REQUIRES_LEGAL_OR_REGULATORY_REVIEW` |
| **Notes** | An Advanced Residential Carpenter's capability to understand temporary support principles is distinct from the authority to engineer temporary works. The level of complexity determines which applies. |

### Scenario ESC-013 — Consumer Asks Work to Continue Despite Unresolved Risk

| Field | Content |
|---|---|
| **Trigger ID** | ESC-013 |
| **Scenario / Condition** | Following a STOP or INVESTIGATE decision, the consumer asks the practitioner to continue work, asserting that the risk is acceptable or that specialist investigation is unnecessary. |
| **Evidence Status** | `USER_DECLARED` (consumer instruction to continue) |
| **Primary Risk** | Continuation in the face of an unresolved safety, authority or regulatory issue; potential harm to consumer, third parties or the practitioner. |
| **Capability Question** | Recognising that consumer preference cannot override safety or authority is a direct expression of Boundary Recognition as a capability domain. |
| **Authority Question** | Consumer instruction does not create authority that the practitioner does not possess and does not remove regulatory obligations. |
| **Initial Decision State** | `STOP` — maintain existing decision; do not resume based on consumer instruction alone |
| **Potential Referral** | None triggered by this scenario |
| **Potential Escalation** | None triggered by this scenario |
| **Consumer Communication Required** | Yes — clearly explain why the boundary exists, what the risk is, and what is required before work can resume |
| **Work Allowed to Continue?** | No change to the prior decision. Work may continue only where the resumption conditions defined in the prior decision have been met. |
| **Resumption Condition** | Resumption conditions from the prior escalation decision must be satisfied; they cannot be waived by consumer instruction |
| **Unresolved Issue** | Where a consumer explicitly declines required action and the risk is a safety risk, the practitioner should consider their position carefully. `REQUIRES_LEGAL_OR_REGULATORY_REVIEW` where contractual consequences arise. |
| **Notes** | This scenario is a direct test of the principle that consumer preference cannot override safety or authority. Withstanding consumer pressure and maintaining a correct decision is a demonstration of professional judgement. |

| Field | Content |
|---|---|
| **Trigger ID** | ESC-015 |

| **Evidence Status** | `USER_DECLARED` (consumer's expanded instructions); legal authority status `UNABLE_TO_VERIFY` without specialist advice |

| **Consumer Communication Required** | Yes — consumer must understand what the scope expansion means, why an authority question arises, and what must be resolved |

| **Notes** | Scope expansion is a common feature of residential renovation. Progressive discovery can also generate scope expansion. The practitioner must identify expansion and refer the authority question — not absorb the expanded scope by default. |

## Proportionality Guidance

This matrix illustrates the framework's proportionate approach:

- Not every scenario that involves a structural element requires an engineer.
- Not every scope expansion requires a registered builder.
- Not every permit uncertainty requires stopping all work.
- `PROCEED_WITH_CONTROL` exists for conditions where documented sequencing or monitoring is sufficient.
- `INVESTIGATE` exists for conditions where more evidence is needed before a more definitive decision can be made.

**Over-escalation is a pilot failure mode as much as under-escalation.** Candidates who routinely escalate in situations where proportionate investigation or controlled continuation would have been appropriate may demonstrate insufficient confidence or calibration, as well as those who proceed without adequate assessment.
