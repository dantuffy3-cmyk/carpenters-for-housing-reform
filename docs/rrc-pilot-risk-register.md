# RRC Pilot Risk Register
**Carpenters for Housing Reform Victoria**
Version: 1.0 — Working Draft
Date: July 2026
Status: WORKING DRAFT — Risks identified for the proposed pilot. Does not use unsupported numerical scoring.

## Purpose
This register records identified risks associated with a proposed Registered Residential Carpenter pilot, including likely causes, potential consequences and possible controls. It does not apply numerical likelihood or impact scoring where no evidentiary basis has been established. All assessments are qualitative, preliminary and intended to support pilot design, consultation and staged government consideration.

## Risk Register Notes
- This register uses qualitative assessment only. Numerical scoring has not been applied.
- All assessments are preliminary and require review during formal pilot design.
- The register should be updated following each stakeholder consultation.
- Residual uncertainty is expected to remain high until pilot design is completed.
