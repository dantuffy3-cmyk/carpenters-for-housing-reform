## Important

This form contains two separate consent sections.

**Part A — Consent to Participate** covers your participation in the pilot assessment.

**Part B — Consent to Publication** covers whether your identified information may be included in published outputs.

You must complete Part A. Part B is optional. You may consent to participate without consenting to publication. Your selection status and assessment outcome are not affected by whether you sign Part B.

Please read the Candidate Information Statement (`docs/pilot-candidate-information-statement-v0.1.md`) before completing this form.

# PART A — CONSENT TO PARTICIPATE

---

## A1. I confirm that:

☐ I have read and understood the Candidate Information Statement.

☐ I have had the opportunity to ask questions before signing this form.

☐ I understand that the pilot tests a capability-assessment framework only.

☐ I understand that participation does not confer registration, licence, builder authority, engineer authority, surveyor authority, licensed-trade authority, contracting rights, or government endorsement.

☐ I understand that a pilot assessment outcome does not create or alter any statutory authority, registration, licence or contracting entitlement.

☐ I understand that my participation is voluntary and that I may withdraw at any time.

☐ I understand that withdrawal does not constitute failure.

---

## A2. I consent to the following as part of my participation:

**A2.1 Participation in assessment activities**

☐ I consent to participate in pilot assessment activities as described in the Candidate Information Statement, including portfolio evidence review, interview and scenario assessment.

**A2.2 Evidence review**

☐ I consent to my portfolio materials and related evidence being reviewed by authorised assessors for the purposes of the pilot assessment.

**A2.3 Interview**

☐ I consent to participating in a structured interview as part of the assessment process.

**A2.4 Scenario assessment**

☐ I consent to participating in scenario-based assessment exercises as part of the pilot.

**A2.5 Recording**

*(This item applies only if recording is used. The pilot coordinator will advise whether recording is proposed.)*

☐ I consent to audio or video recording of my assessment activities, to be used for assessor calibration, quality review and audit purposes only.

☐ I do not consent to recording.

*(If you do not consent to recording, the pilot coordinator will advise whether participation can proceed without recording.)*

**A2.6 Anonymised use of pilot results**

☐ I consent to my assessment evidence and outcomes being used in anonymised or pseudonymised form in pilot reports, governance records and aggregate analysis.

**A2.7 Aggregate reporting**

☐ I consent to my assessment data being included in aggregate pilot findings, where no individual can be identified from the published output.

**A2.8 Contact for follow-up**

☐ I consent to being contacted by the pilot coordinator for follow-up questions related to my assessment, or for evaluation of the pilot process.

---

## A3. Privacy notice acknowledgment

☐ I acknowledge that I have been informed about how my personal information is collected, stored and used under the pilot Privacy and Data Handling Plan (`docs/pilot-privacy-and-data-handling-plan-v0.1.md`).

☐ I understand that my identifiable information is held securely and accessed only by authorised pilot personnel.

☐ I understand that the privacy arrangements are a working draft and that final privacy obligations require legal and governance review before live pilot commencement.

---

## A4. Withdrawal process

☐ I understand that I may withdraw from the pilot at any time by notifying the pilot coordinator in writing.

☐ I understand that evidence already incorporated into anonymised aggregate analysis may not always be technically removable, and that my identifiable records will be handled according to this consent form and the Privacy and Data Handling Plan.

☐ I understand that withdrawal does not constitute failure and will not be characterised as such.

---

## A5. Complaints

☐ I understand that I may raise a complaint about any aspect of the pilot through the Complaints and Review Process.

☐ I understand that raising a complaint does not automatically change my assessment result.
