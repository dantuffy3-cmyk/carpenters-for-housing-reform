## Purpose

This declaration records potential conflicts of interest held by external reviewers.

Conflict does not automatically disqualify a reviewer.

The purpose of this declaration is transparency and appropriate interpretation of reviewer evidence.

Reviewer findings will be assessed in light of declared conflicts. Conflicts that are not declared and later emerge may affect the weight given to the reviewer's findings.
