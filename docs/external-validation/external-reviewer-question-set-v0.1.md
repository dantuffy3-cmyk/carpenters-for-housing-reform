## Instructions for Reviewers

These questions are designed to invite critical scrutiny of the Advanced Residential Carpentry reform framework.

Reviewers are asked to challenge the framework, not endorse it.

For each question, reviewers may:

- answer directly;
- explain their reasoning;
- cite evidence;
- identify an evidence gap;
- decline to answer (with reason); or
- recommend that the question be put to a specialist.

There are no required answers. Material objections are as valuable as supportive views.
