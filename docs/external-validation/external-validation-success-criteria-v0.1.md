# External Validation Success Criteria — Advanced Residential Carpentry v0.1

**Status:** Working Draft.
**Prepared by:** Carpenters for Housing Reform Victoria
**Version:** 0.1
**Date:** August 2026

---

## Purpose

This document defines what constitutes a successful external validation of the Advanced Residential Carpentry reform framework.

**Success does not mean reviewer agreement.**

Success is defined in terms of the quality, independence and completeness of the challenge process — not the conclusions reviewers reach.

---

## Success Criteria

External validation is considered successful when all of the following are met:

### 1. Credible Reviewer Independence
Reviewers have been selected from relevant perspectives and have no undeclared conflicts that would materially compromise the independence of their findings.

### 2. Relevant Perspectives Exposed
The framework has been reviewed by a sufficient range of perspectives to surface the material objections most likely to arise from each relevant professional and consumer standpoint. Complete coverage of all reviewer categories is not required; material gaps in perspective must be noted.

### 3. Material Objections Captured
Every material objection that arose during the review process has been recorded in the External Reviewer Feedback Record and External Validation Evidence Register.

### 4. Evidence Gaps Identified
Reviewers have had the opportunity to identify evidence gaps, and any evidence gaps identified have been recorded and classified.

### 5. Disagreements Preserved
Disagreements between reviewers and the initiative have been recorded and not resolved by suppression or mischaracterisation.

### 6. Responses Documented
The initiative has responded to each material objection. Responses are recorded and traceable.

### 7. Framework Changes Traceable
Any changes made to the framework in response to reviewer findings are documented, with the reviewer finding, the initiative's reasoning and the change made all visible.

### 8. Unresolved Matters Visible
Matters that could not be resolved during external validation are identified in the External Validation Closeout Record (`external-validation-closeout-record-v0.1.md`) and remain open for consideration in subsequent stages.

### 9. No Suppression of Adverse Findings
Adverse findings, null findings and unresolved objections are present in the Evidence Register and have not been deleted, hidden or materially distorted.

### 10. Sufficient Challenge to Justify the Next Decision
The range and quality of challenge received is sufficient to support a reasoned decision about whether to proceed toward pilot preparation, conduct further validation, revise the framework or stop.

## Possible Validation Outcomes

These outcomes reflect the findings of external validation. They confer no statutory authority and do not bind government or any regulatory body.

| Outcome | Meaning |
|---|---|
| READY_FOR_GOVERNED_PILOT_PREPARATION | External validation did not identify unresolved material objections. The framework is considered sufficiently robust to proceed toward pilot preparation. |
| READY_WITH_REFINEMENTS | External validation identified refinements that have been made. The framework is considered suitable for pilot preparation with those refinements in place. |
| FURTHER_EXTERNAL_REVIEW_REQUIRED | External validation identified perspectives or issues that require additional external challenge before a decision on pilot preparation can be made. |
| MATERIAL_FRAMEWORK_REVISION_REQUIRED | External validation identified one or more material objections that require substantive revision of the framework before it can be considered ready for pilot preparation. |
| SPECIALIST_LEGAL_OR_REGULATORY_REVIEW_REQUIRED | External validation identified legal, regulatory or jurisdictional issues that require specialist advice before the framework can proceed. |
| SPECIALIST_TECHNICAL_REVIEW_REQUIRED | External validation identified technical (engineering, construction, assessment-design or other) issues that require specialist review before the framework can proceed. |
| INSUFFICIENT_EXTERNAL_VALIDATION | The validation process did not achieve sufficient reviewer coverage or challenge quality to support a reliable conclusion. |
| VALIDATION_IDENTIFIED_MATERIAL_OBJECTION | External validation has identified one or more material objections that have not been resolved and must be addressed before proceeding. |
| DO_NOT_PROCEED_TO_PILOT_AT_THIS_STAGE | External validation findings, in combination, indicate that the framework should not proceed to pilot at this time. |

None of these outcomes require that formal recognition be adopted or rejected. All six implementation pathways (A–F) remain open regardless of validation outcome.

No formal recognition / status quo remains a legitimate outcome at every stage.

---

## What Validation Does Not Establish

Completion of external validation, and a positive validation outcome, does not establish:

- that formal recognition is required;
- that the Registered Residential Carpenter model should be adopted;
- that government will endorse or fund any aspect of the initiative;
- that the pilot will produce evidence of regulatory need;
- that assessment outcomes will be legally recognised;
- that any statutory vehicle is appropriate.

Validation establishes only that the framework has been subjected to credible independent challenge and that the initiative has responded transparently.
