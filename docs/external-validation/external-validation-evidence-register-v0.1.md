# External Validation Evidence Register — Advanced Residential Carpentry v0.1

**Status:** Working Draft — template for ongoing completion during external validation.
**Prepared by:** Carpenters for Housing Reform Victoria
**Version:** 0.1
**Date:** August 2026

---

## Purpose

The External Validation Evidence Register is the primary record of all material reviewer comments, their classification, the initiative's response and their final disposition.

Every material reviewer comment must be traceable through this register.

Adverse findings, null findings and unresolved objections must not be deleted.

---

## Disposition Definitions

| Disposition | Meaning |
|---|---|
| ACCEPTED | The reviewer's finding is accepted. A change has been made or a gap acknowledged. |
| PARTIALLY_ACCEPTED | The reviewer's finding is partly accepted. Some elements are acknowledged; others are not accepted with reason. |
| NOT_ACCEPTED_WITH_REASON | The reviewer's finding is not accepted. The initiative has documented its reasoning. |
| FURTHER_EVIDENCE_REQUIRED | The finding identifies an evidence gap that must be investigated before a disposition can be reached. |
| FRAMEWORK_CHANGE_REQUIRED | The finding identifies a problem that requires a substantive change to the architecture. |
| REFERRED_FOR_SPECIALIST_REVIEW | The finding requires specialist (legal, regulatory, engineering, insurance or other) input before the initiative can respond. |
| DEFERRED | The finding is noted but its resolution is deferred to a later stage. The reason for deferral is recorded. |
| UNRESOLVED | The finding cannot currently be resolved. It is retained as an unresolved matter in the validation closeout record. |

## Adverse Finding Retention Control

All entries with dispositions of NOT_ACCEPTED_WITH_REASON, UNRESOLVED or FRAMEWORK_CHANGE_REQUIRED must be:

- retained in this register;
- included in the validation closeout record;
- visible in the decision whether to proceed toward pilot preparation.

No entry may be deleted. Errors in prior entries should be corrected by adding a correction note in a new row, not by deleting or overwriting the original entry.

---

## Cross-Reference to Feedback Record

Each evidence register entry that corresponds to a Feedback Record entry must include the Feedback Record ID (FBK-xxx) in the cross-reference field.

Entries may exist in the Evidence Register without a corresponding Feedback Record entry where the reviewer's comment was brief and captured directly.
