# Advanced Residential Carpentry — External Validation One-Page Summary

**Status:** Working Draft — for reviewer orientation only.
**Prepared by:** Carpenters for Housing Reform Victoria
**Version:** 0.1
**Date:** August 2026

---

## What is being tested

The initiative has developed an **Advanced Residential Carpentry capability framework**: a practitioner-function description, seven capability domains, an assessment architecture, an assessor-calibration architecture, and a pilot governance model.

External validation tests whether that architecture is credible, whether the practitioner function is genuinely distinguishable, whether the assessment design can produce reliable evidence, and whether material objections exist that must be resolved before a governed pilot proceeds.

---

## What is NOT predetermined

No decision has been made that:

- a new licence or registration class is required;
- the Registered Residential Carpenter model will be adopted;
- DB-L Carpentry reform is preferable;
- formal recognition is necessary or proportionate;
- government will endorse or adopt the framework.

**No formal recognition / status quo remains a legitimate outcome.**

Six implementation options remain open (A through F), including no additional formal recognition.

---

## What reviewers are being asked to do

Reviewers are asked to **challenge the framework**, not endorse it.

Specifically:

- identify whether the practitioner function is genuinely distinct;
- identify whether any capability domains are missing, overstated or incorrectly bounded;
- identify whether the assessment design can distinguish competent from advanced performance;
- identify whether engineering, builder, surveyor and trade boundaries are adequately protected;
- identify evidence gaps;
- identify the strongest argument against proceeding;
- record material objections.

---

## Key protections

- Dan Sonsie may explain practitioner context but may not direct reviewer conclusions.
- Reviewers may decline publication of their findings.
- Publication consent is separate from participation consent.
- Anonymous and de-identified review is available where appropriate.
- Adverse findings, null findings and unresolved objections will be retained.
- Reviewer support is not treated as proof.

---

## What external validation means

External validation does not mean reviewers agree with the proposal.

It means the framework has been exposed to credible independent challenge and material objections have been recorded, investigated and responded to transparently.

---

## What happens to adverse findings

Adverse findings, null findings and unresolved objections are recorded in the External Validation Evidence Register and must not be deleted or suppressed.

Each material objection receives an initiative response and a disposition (accepted, not accepted with reason, further evidence required, etc.).

Unresolved material objections are visible in the final validation closeout record and inform the decision whether to proceed toward pilot preparation.
