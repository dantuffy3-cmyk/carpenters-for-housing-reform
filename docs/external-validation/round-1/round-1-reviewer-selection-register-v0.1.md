## Purpose

This is the governance register for Round 1 external reviewer selection.

It records the status of each potential reviewer position, tracks screening and approval progress and enforces the governance control that no reviewer may be contacted without explicit Dan Sonsie approval.

This register does not contain invented names. Placeholder rows are provided for the three Round 1 reviewer positions.

---

## Governance Control

**No reviewer may move to CONTACTED status unless Dan Sonsie has explicitly approved the person for approach.**

This control is absolute for Round 1.

Screening, suitability assessment and recommendation may be completed by others, but the decision to approach any individual reviewer requires explicit Dan Sonsie approval recorded in this register.

## Register Notes

### Round 1 composition

Round 1 consists of three reviewer perspectives:

1. Experienced residential renovation carpenter — testing whether the practitioner function is genuinely distinguishable in practice
2. Registered domestic builder — testing whether builder responsibility remains properly bounded
3. Structural engineer — testing whether engineering authority remains protected

### Round 1 limits

This register governs Round 1 only.

Potential later perspectives — building surveyor, competency specialist, consumer representative, insurance or risk specialist, regulatory or policy reviewer — are available for subsequent rounds and are not tracked here.

### Reviewer / assessor separation

Reviewer and assessor roles are initially treated as separate.

An entry in this register does not create eligibility for a future pilot assessor role. Any such transition requires a separate and explicit governance decision.

### Privacy

This register is held in the public repository.

If real candidate names are entered into this register, consideration must be given to whether:

- the register or the relevant rows should be moved to internal-only governance storage or a private repository;
- a de-identified public version is maintained instead.

This is an unresolved implementation control. No privacy solution has been predetermined.

Do not record personal contact details (phone numbers, personal email addresses) in this register.
