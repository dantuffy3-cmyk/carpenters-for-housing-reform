## Draft Invitation

---

*[Date]*

Dear *[Name]*,

**Invitation to participate as an independent external reviewer — Advanced Residential Carpentry framework**

I am writing on behalf of Carpenters for Housing Reform Victoria to invite your consideration of an independent external reviewer role.

**About the initiative**

Carpenters for Housing Reform Victoria is examining a specific policy question: whether advanced residential renovation carpentry represents a distinguishable practitioner function within Victoria's building regulatory framework that may warrant a defined recognition mechanism.

The initiative is evidence-led and consumer-focused. It does not prejudge a regulatory outcome. No formal recognition outcome is predetermined. The status quo — the existing regulatory framework without any new recognition mechanism — remains a legitimate outcome at every stage of the work.

The initiative is not government-sponsored. It does not represent the Department of Transport and Planning, the Building and Plumbing Commission or any regulatory body. No government body has endorsed it.

**Why we are approaching you**

*[WHY THIS REVIEWER'S PROFESSIONAL PERSPECTIVE IS RELEVANT]*

We are approaching you specifically because your professional experience and perspective places you in a position to subject the framework to credible independent challenge — not to endorse it.

**What we are asking you to do**

We are not asking you to agree with the framework.

We are asking you to read the material provided, apply your professional judgement, and tell us where you think the framework is wrong, overstated, unsafe, unclear, or insufficiently bounded.

Adverse findings are welcome. Critical assessments that identify genuine weaknesses in the framework are of equal or greater value than supportive ones. All material findings — including disagreement, objection and qualification — are retained in the record, investigated and responded to transparently.

**Material you would receive**

If you agree to participate, you would receive:

- the Advanced Residential Carpentry capability framework;
- a structured reviewer question set designed to invite criticism;
- a conflict of interest declaration form; and
- relevant supporting documents.

The total volume of material is approximately *[estimated page count to be confirmed]*.

**Likely time commitment**

The likely time commitment for a Round 1 reviewer is approximately *[estimated hours — to be confirmed]* hours, including reading time, response preparation and, if you are willing, a brief follow-up exchange.

This is an initial estimate only and will be confirmed before you are asked to commit.

**Your identity and publication**

Your participation as a reviewer, and your individual findings, will not be attributed to you publicly without your explicit written consent.

If you prefer to remain unidentified in any public record, that preference will be respected. A de-identified record of material findings may still be maintained for governance purposes.

You may withdraw from the process at any time before your formal response is submitted.

**Conflict of interest**

Before confirming participation, you will be asked to complete a short conflict declaration. This records any relationship, financial interest or prior public position that could affect, or be perceived to affect, your independence as a reviewer.

A declared conflict does not automatically prevent participation. How it is managed will depend on its nature and materiality.

**What you are not being asked to do**

You are not being asked to:

- endorse the initiative;
- publicly support any regulatory proposal;
- agree that a new recognition mechanism is needed;
- provide a testimonial;
- speak on behalf of any profession or organisation; or
- engage with government or regulatory bodies on the initiative's behalf.

**Next step**

If you are interested in hearing more, or in principle open to participating, I would welcome a brief conversation at your convenience. You are of course also welcome to decline — and I appreciate that your time is limited.

Please reply to this message at your convenience. There is no obligation.

Yours sincerely,

Dan Sonsie
Carpenters for Housing Reform Victoria
