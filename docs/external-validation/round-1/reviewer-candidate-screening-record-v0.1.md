---

## Purpose

This document is a reusable template for recording the screening of individual candidates being considered as Round 1 external reviewers.

One record should be completed for each candidate under consideration.

No candidate may be contacted unless Dan Sonsie has explicitly authorised outreach.

---

## Governance Control

**CONTACTED status is not available in this screening record unless Dan Sonsie has explicitly approved outreach for this specific candidate.**

Approved screening decisions progress through:

`IDENTIFIED → SCREENING_REQUIRED → SUITABLE / SUITABLE_WITH_DECLARED_CONFLICT / NOT_SUITABLE / DEFER → APPROVED_FOR_APPROACH`

Only after explicit Dan Sonsie approval of approach may a candidate's status progress further.

---

## Controlled Screening Decisions

| Status | Meaning |
|---|---|
| `IDENTIFIED` | Candidate has been identified as a potential reviewer. No screening completed. |
| `SCREENING_REQUIRED` | Candidate has been identified. Screening information is being gathered. |
| `SUITABLE` | Candidate appears suitable on available information. No material conflict identified. |
| `SUITABLE_WITH_DECLARED_CONFLICT` | Candidate appears suitable but has a declared conflict that must be managed. |
| `NOT_SUITABLE` | Candidate is not suitable for Round 1 on current information. |
| `DEFER` | Candidate may be suitable for a later round but should not be approached in Round 1. |
| `APPROVED_FOR_APPROACH` | Dan Sonsie has explicitly approved outreach to this candidate. |

## Privacy Note

This screening record template may contain personal information about individuals.

If real individuals are entered into this record, consideration must be given to whether the completed record belongs in:

- internal-only governance storage;
- a private repository;
- or a de-identified public register.

Do not record unnecessary personal contact details (phone numbers, personal email addresses, home addresses) in the public repository.

This is an unresolved implementation control. No privacy solution has been predetermined.
