## Governance Notice

> **A validation closeout recommendation is an initiative governance recommendation only. It does not constitute government approval, regulatory endorsement, statutory recognition or a decision to create any practitioner category.**

> **A positive validation outcome does not establish that formal recognition is required.**

Government retains full discretion over any statutory or policy decision. The question of an appropriate statutory vehicle, if any, remains a later government question. This record documents the outcome of an internal initiative governance process.

---

## Purpose

This document is completed at the close of each external validation round.

Its purpose is to:

- record the reviewer perspectives engaged and any coverage gaps;
- summarise material findings and their dispositions;
- identify unresolved matters and the specialist input required to address them;
- document framework changes made or not made, and the rationale;
- assess reviewer independence and the quality of challenge received;
- record a recommended validation outcome; and
- determine whether the framework should proceed, be revised, be subject to further review or stop.

This record is consistent with the External Validation Process (`external-validation-process-v0.1.md`) and the External Validation Success Criteria (`external-validation-success-criteria-v0.1.md`).

- [ ] **SPECIALIST REVIEW REQUIRED** — One or more findings require specialist input (engineering, legal, insurance, regulatory) before the framework can progress.
