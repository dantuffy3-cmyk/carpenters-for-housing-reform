# Pilot Role and Responsibility Matrix — v0.1

**Status:** WORKING DRAFT

**Prepared by:** Carpenters for Housing Reform Victoria
**Version:** 0.1
**Date:** August 2026

---

## Purpose

This matrix defines the roles in the ARC capability-assessment pilot, the responsibilities of each role, the decisions each role may and may not make, their escalation path, and the records each role must create.

The matrix does not assign personnel to roles. Personnel assignments require a separate governance decision recorded in the Pilot Decision Register.

---

## Important — Founder and Assessor Roles

Dan Sonsie holds the role of Pilot Sponsor and Initiative Lead. This is distinct from any assessor or independent reviewer role.

Dan Sonsie does not automatically hold assessor authority and should not assess candidates unless separately appointed through a documented independent governance process.

Where Dan Sonsie is involved in governance decisions, that involvement must be recorded and those decisions must be reviewable.

## Role Descriptions

---

### Role 1 — Pilot Sponsor / Initiative Lead

**Purpose:** Accountable for the pilot purpose, governance framework and strategic decisions.

**Responsibilities:**
- Approve the governance plan and amendments.
- Appoint the pilot coordinator and other governance roles.
- Approve changes to pilot scope, design or framework.
- Authorise pause or stop decisions where time permits.
- Maintain accountability for the pilot to government and stakeholders.
- Ensure conflicts are managed.
- Ensure governance decisions are recorded.

**Decisions this role may make:**
- Approve or amend the governance plan.
- Approve cohort size and design changes.
- Approve pause, stop or redesign.
- Approve escalation outcomes.
- Appoint governance roles.

**Decisions this role may NOT make:**
- Assess individual candidate capability (unless separately appointed as an assessor through an independent process).
- Assign individual candidates to the eligible pool unilaterally.
- Override a conflict recusal without recording the decision and reason.
- Publish identifiable participant data without consent.

**Escalation path:** The pilot sponsor is the final escalation point within the pilot governance structure. External escalation may go to legal or governance advisors, or to a government or regulatory body where formally involved.

**Records this role must create:**
- Approvals of governance plan and amendments (Pilot Decision Register).
- Decisions to pause, stop or redesign the pilot (Pilot Decision Register).
- Appointment of governance roles (Pilot Decision Register).

---

### Role 2 — Pilot Coordinator

**Purpose:** Day-to-day operational management of the pilot.

**Responsibilities:**
- Manage recruitment logistics and communication with applicants.
- Conduct or coordinate eligibility screening.
- Collect and file consent forms and conflict declarations.
- Assign participant IDs.
- Coordinate assessor assignments (in consultation with calibration chair).
- Maintain the Participant Register and Recruitment and Selection Log.
- Record incidents and coordinate incident responses.
- Receive and process complaints.
- Manage communications with all participant categories.
- Maintain the evidence custodianship function or delegate to the evidence custodian.

**Decisions this role may make:**
- Apply controlled eligibility reason codes to applicants.
- Assign participant IDs.
- Schedule assessment activities.
- Escalate conflicts and incidents.

**Decisions this role may NOT make:**
- Make candidate eligibility decisions without applying the documented eligibility criteria.
- Override conflict recusals.
- Change assessment outcomes.
- Approve publication of identifiable participant data.
- Pause or stop the pilot (except in immediate safety situations where the sponsor is not available).

**Escalation path:** Pilot Sponsor.

**Records this role must create:**
- Participant Register entries.
- Recruitment and Selection Log entries.
- Consent form and conflict declaration filing records.
- Incident reports (initial recording).
- Complaint intake records.

---

### Role 3 — Eligibility Reviewer

**Purpose:** Apply eligibility criteria to applicants and recommend inclusion or exclusion from the eligible pool.

**Responsibilities:**
- Review EOI submissions against documented inclusion and exclusion criteria.
- Verify evidence of qualification and experience.
- Contact referees or employers where consented and required.
- Record eligibility outcomes with controlled reason codes.
- Escalate uncertain cases to the pilot coordinator.

**Decisions this role may make:**
- Apply eligibility reason codes to individual applicants.
- Request additional evidence from an applicant.

**Decisions this role may NOT make:**
- Select candidates for the pilot (selection is a separate decision by the selection panel).
- Make final decisions about conflicts.
- Override documented eligibility criteria.

**Escalation path:** Pilot Coordinator.

**Records this role must create:**
- Eligibility review notes per applicant (filed with Participant Register).
- Evidence verification records.

---

### Role 4 — Assessor

**Purpose:** Apply the assessment framework to assigned candidates and generate assessment findings.

**Responsibilities:**
- Complete assessor calibration before conducting assessments.
- Declare conflicts before each candidate assignment.
- Apply the assessment framework consistently.
- Conduct interviews, scenario assessments and portfolio reviews as assigned.
- Record observations, evidence and assessment findings.
- Escalate concerns about safety, evidence integrity or boundary issues.
- Participate in calibration and moderation processes.

**Decisions this role may make:**
- Apply the assessment framework to individual candidates.
- Record assessment findings and observations.
- Escalate safety or integrity concerns immediately.

**Decisions this role may NOT make:**
- Modify the assessment framework or criteria unilaterally.
- Disclose candidate identity to other participants without authority.
- Share assessment scenario details outside the pilot.
- Make registration or licensing determinations.
- Assess a candidate where a recusal is required.

**Escalation path:** Calibration Chair, then Pilot Coordinator.

**Records this role must create:**
- Assessment records per candidate (using Participant ID).
- Conflict declaration (before each assignment).
- Incident reports where required.

---

### Role 5 — Second Assessor

**Purpose:** Provide an independent assessment of assigned candidates for calibration and quality assurance purposes.

**Responsibilities:** As per Assessor (Role 4), conducting an independent assessment without access to the primary assessor's findings (until moderation).

**Decisions this role may make:** As per Assessor (Role 4).

**Decisions this role may NOT make:** As per Assessor (Role 4). Must not view the primary assessor's findings before completing independent assessment.

**Escalation path:** Calibration Chair, then Pilot Coordinator.

**Records this role must create:** As per Assessor (Role 4).

---

### Role 6 — Calibration Chair

**Purpose:** Manage assessor calibration, consistency and moderation.

**Responsibilities:**
- Plan and conduct assessor calibration sessions before the pilot commences.
- Review calibration results and identify divergence.
- Manage moderation processes where assessors disagree on a candidate finding.
- Advise the pilot coordinator on assessor readiness.
- Flag systematic divergence to the pilot sponsor.

**Decisions this role may make:**
- Determine assessor readiness for the pilot.
- Convene moderation discussions.
- Record calibration outcomes.

**Decisions this role may NOT make:**
- Override an individual assessor's documented finding without a moderation process.
- Approve assessors who have not completed calibration.
- Make governance decisions outside calibration scope.

**Escalation path:** Pilot Sponsor.

**Records this role must create:**
- Calibration session records.
- Calibration outcome summaries.
- Moderation records where applicable.

---

### Role 7 — Technical / Governance Reviewer

**Purpose:** Provide specialist review of assessment methodology, governance or technical content where required.

**Responsibilities:**
- Review specific aspects of the framework or assessment content on request.
- Provide written advice or observations.
- Declare conflicts before review commences.

**Decisions this role may make:**
- Provide advisory findings or recommendations.

**Decisions this role may NOT make:**
- Make candidate eligibility or assessment outcome decisions.
- Change the framework without a formal governance process.

**Escalation path:** Pilot Coordinator or Pilot Sponsor.

**Records this role must create:**
- Written review outputs or advisory notes.
- Conflict declaration.

---

### Role 8 — Consumer-Test Facilitator

**Purpose:** Coordinate any consumer-participant involvement in role-comprehension or communication testing.

**Responsibilities:**
- Recruit and brief consumer participants.
- Conduct comprehension or communication testing.
- Record findings.
- Ensure no consumer is exposed to unsafe building work.
- Manage consent and privacy for consumer participants.

**Decisions this role may make:**
- Determine consumer test session logistics.
- Stop a consumer test session if a concern arises.

**Decisions this role may NOT make:**
- Expose consumers to live or potentially unsafe building activity.
- Make candidate assessment decisions.

**Escalation path:** Pilot Coordinator.

**Records this role must create:**
- Consumer-test session records.
- Consent records for consumer participants.

---

### Role 9 — Evidence Custodian

**Purpose:** Maintain secure custody of all pilot records and manage access controls.

**Responsibilities:**
- Maintain secure storage of all identifiable pilot records.
- Control access to participant data.
- Implement anonymisation and pseudonymisation protocols.
- Manage evidence associated with withdrawals.
- Handle privacy incidents.
- Respond to correction requests.

**Decisions this role may make:**
- Grant or deny access to identifiable data in accordance with the Privacy and Data Handling Plan.
- Trigger privacy incident procedures.

**Decisions this role may NOT make:**
- Destroy records outside the retention and destruction process.
- Share identifiable data without consent or a documented governance decision.

**Escalation path:** Pilot Coordinator, then Pilot Sponsor.

**Records this role must create:**
- Access log for identifiable records.
- Privacy incident records.
- Destruction records at end of retention period.

---

### Role 10 — Privacy / Governance Contact

**Purpose:** Act as the point of contact for participant privacy and governance queries.

**Responsibilities:**
- Respond to participant queries about their data and rights.
- Coordinate with the evidence custodian.
- Ensure the Privacy and Data Handling Plan is followed.
- Escalate incidents or unresolved questions.

**Escalation path:** Pilot Sponsor, then legal advisor where required.

**Records this role must create:**
- Privacy query log.
- Escalation records.

---

### Role 11 — Observer

**Purpose:** Observe pilot governance and assessment processes for information or review purposes.

**Responsibilities:**
- Observe only — no interference with assessment activities.
- Comply with the Observer Protocol (`docs/pilot-observer-protocol-v0.1.md`).
- Complete an observer confidentiality agreement.
- Declare conflicts.

**Decisions this role may make:**
- Ask questions through agreed processes defined in the Observer Protocol.

**Decisions this role may NOT make:**
- Interfere with or direct assessment activities.
- Access identifiable participant data.
- Represent their observation as endorsement.

**Escalation path:** Pilot Coordinator.

**Records this role must create:**
- Observer participation is recorded by the pilot coordinator in the Pilot Decision Register.

---

### Role 12 — Participant (Candidate Carpenter)

**Purpose:** Undergo the capability assessment.

**Responsibilities:**
- Provide accurate information in the EOI and evidence submission.
- Participate honestly in assessment activities.
- Comply with confidentiality requirements in relation to assessment scenario content.
- Notify the pilot coordinator of any concerns or conflicts.

**Decisions this role may make:**
- Withdraw from the pilot at any time.
- Raise a complaint.

**Records this role must create:**
- No records required of the participant themselves, other than consent form and conflict declaration.
