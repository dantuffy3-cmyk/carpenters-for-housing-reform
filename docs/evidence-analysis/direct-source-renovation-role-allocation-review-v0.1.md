---

## Purpose

This review tests what **directly verified authoritative sources** show about who:

- observes,
- interprets,
- stops/holds,
- reports,
- refers/escalates,
- authorises specialist input,
- authorises scope change,
- authorises work resumption,

when concealed or changed conditions are discovered in residential renovation or existing-building work.

The third-party scout report is treated as **THIRD_PARTY_SEARCH_LEAD** only.

## Source Controls and Verification Constraints

1. Unit code/title and quote claims were only retained where directly tieable to official source records.
2. Where runtime host-resolution limits prevented direct page fetch, results were retained only with traceable official URLs and marked with reduced confidence.
3. Contradictory retrievals were not forced into a positive finding; they were classified **UNVERIFIED** or **DO_NOT_USE**.
4. Recognition/participation evidence was kept separate from statutory or contractual authority.
