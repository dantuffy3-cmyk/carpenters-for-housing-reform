# Functional Distinctiveness Evidence Baseline

**Status:** WORKING DRAFT  
**Date:** August 2026  
**Scope:** Functional distinctiveness baseline for the Advanced Residential Carpentry (ARC) policy question in Victoria.

---

## Purpose Notice

This baseline tests whether currently available public evidence establishes a distinct residual renovation function that is materially separate from ordinary or experienced carpentry, site supervision, DB-L Carpentry, builder responsibility, engineering, surveying or licensed-trade functions.

This is not an advocacy document and does not predetermine regulatory reform.

## Evidence Positioning Controls

### POSITIVE_EVIDENCE_FOR_DISTINCTIVENESS

Positive primary-source evidence for a distinct residual function remains limited.

### EVIDENCE_GAPS

The statement that Domains 6 and 7 are not clearly evidenced in CPC30220 public material is treated as an evidence gap, not positive evidence of distinctiveness.

### CASE_EVIDENCE

Five practitioner cases are retained as **CASE_EVIDENCE_ONLY**. They may illustrate diagnostic judgement, sequencing under uncertainty, escalation/referral behaviour and boundary recognition pressures.

### ALTERNATIVE_EXPLANATIONS

Alternative explanations remain credible, including overlap with experienced carpentry practice, site management quality, builder-led coordination controls, and existing pathway effects (including DB-L Carpentry).

---

## Residual Function Finding

**Result:** **PARTIAL_RESIDUAL_FUNCTION**

The residual function is narrowly framed as a **structured diagnostic judgement and explicit boundary-recognition capability hypothesis** in existing-building renovation contexts with progressive discovery.

This does not establish a fully distinct occupation, and it does not exclude overlap with ordinary carpentry, experienced carpentry, site supervision, DB-L Carpentry, builder responsibility, engineering, surveying or licensed trades.

---

## Boundary-Recognition Wording Control

This baseline does not claim an existing formal boundary-recognition competency.  
Where relevant, the document uses **STRUCTURED_BOUNDARY_RECOGNITION_HYPOTHESIS** or equivalent wording (explicit boundary-recognition capability hypothesis).
