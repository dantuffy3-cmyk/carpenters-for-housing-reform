## Important

This score sheet is a calibration instrument only. Scores recorded here are assessor calibration records, not candidate assessment outcomes.

This document uses the 0–4 scoring rubric from Section 6 of the Advanced Residential Carpentry Capability Assessment Framework v0.1. No new scoring system is introduced.

Scores without written rationale are invalid and must be returned for completion.

## Part 4 — Domain Scoring

Use the following rubric for all domains:

| Score | Code | Meaning |
|---|---|---|
| 4 | `DEMONSTRATED_ADVANCED` | Advanced capability demonstrated. Reasoning clear, evidence-based, proportionate and integrating. Boundary recognition explicit and accurate. |
| 3 | `DEMONSTRATED_COMPETENT` | Sound capability demonstrated. Reasoning sound. Minor gaps in integration or depth but no risk indicator. |
| 2 | `PARTIALLY_DEMONSTRATED` | Some capability demonstrated with material gaps, inconsistencies or weaknesses requiring attention. |
| 1 | `NOT_DEMONSTRATED` | Required capability not demonstrated. Evidence absent, insufficient or contradicted. |
| 0 | `UNSAFE_OR_BOUNDARY_FAILURE` | Unsafe behaviour, reasoning or decision-making, or crossing of a critical professional boundary. Triggers critical-fail review. |

## Part 6 — Critical-Fail Assessment

| Field | Response |
|---|---|

| **Critical-fail rule(s) relied upon** | Rule 1 / Rule 2 / Rule 3 / Rule 4 / Rule 5 / Rule 6 / Rule 7 / None |

**Critical-fail rules (from Assessment Framework v0.1 Section 6.4):**

1. Knowingly continuing unsafe work in a scenario or exercise
2. Failing to recognise a clear engineering boundary when present
3. Failing to recognise a clear builder authority, permit or licensed-trade boundary when present
4. Treating major uncertainty as confirmation of safety
5. Fabricating, misrepresenting or substantially mischaracterising evidence
6. Recommending a course of action outside lawful authority as if it were within authority
7. Failing to communicate material risk to the consumer in two or more separate assessment components
