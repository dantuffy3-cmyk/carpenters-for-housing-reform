# Pilot Candidate Recruitment Framework v0.1

**Status:** WORKING DRAFT

**Prepared by:** Carpenters for Housing Reform Victoria
**Version:** 0.1
**Date:** August 2026

---

## Important Notice

This framework defines the recruitment approach for a **future controlled pilot** of the Advanced Residential Carpentry (ARC) capability-assessment framework. It does not commence live recruitment. It does not create statutory authority. Submitting an expression of interest or being recruited does not confer registration, licence, builder authority, or any other statutory entitlement.

---

## 1. Recruitment Purpose

Recruitment for the ARC pilot aims to identify candidate carpenters who:

- have relevant residential renovation experience;
- represent a useful variation of experience types and project categories;
- are willing to participate voluntarily under governed conditions;
- are able to provide verifiable evidence of their work.

The purpose of recruitment is to support a valid and informative capability-assessment pilot — not to identify candidates expected to succeed, not to reward reform supporters, and not to confirm a predetermined outcome.

---

## 2. Recruitment Channels

Recruitment may use any combination of the following channels:

| Channel | Description |
|---|---|
| Direct practitioner invitation | Targeted invitation to known practitioners from diverse backgrounds |
| Industry association circulation | Circulation through carpentry, building or trade associations |
| Public expression of interest | Open EOI process accessible to any eligible practitioner |
| Training provider referral | Referral from registered training organisations delivering carpentry qualifications |
| Professional network outreach | Outreach through professional networks, forums or online communities |

Recruitment must not rely solely on the founder's personal network. Channels should be selected to reach practitioners with a range of experience types, geographic locations, and employment contexts.

---

## 3. Eligibility Screening

All applicants must be screened for eligibility before being considered for selection.

Eligibility screening assesses:

- whether the applicant holds a recognised carpentry qualification or documented apprenticeship equivalent;
- whether the applicant has verifiable residential renovation experience;
- whether the applicant is able to provide evidence of their work (portfolio, records, references or equivalent);
- whether the applicant is able to consent and participate voluntarily;
- whether any conflict of interest is present that would prevent participation.

Eligibility screening is conducted by the eligibility reviewer role as defined in the Role and Responsibility Matrix (`docs/pilot-role-responsibility-matrix-v0.1.md`).

---

## 4. Inclusion Criteria

An applicant may be included in the eligible pool if they:

- hold a recognised carpentry qualification (e.g. Certificate III in Carpentry) or can demonstrate an apprenticeship or equivalent pathway;
- have verifiable experience in residential renovation work (type and volume to be determined by pilot design; see Unresolved Question [Q-021]);
- are willing to provide portfolio evidence or equivalent documentation;
- consent to the verification of their evidence;
- have no unmanageable conflict of interest;
- are available to participate within the pilot timeframe;
- have provided a completed Expression of Interest (`docs/pilot-candidate-expression-of-interest-v0.1.md`).

---

## 5. Exclusion Criteria

An applicant must be excluded if they:

- do not hold a recognised qualification or equivalent pathway;
- cannot provide verifiable evidence of renovation experience;
- have an unmanageable conflict of interest (e.g. direct personal or commercial relationship with the assessor assigned to them, where recusal is not practicable);
- are unable or unwilling to provide informed consent;
- have submitted materially false or misleading information.

An applicant excluded under these criteria is assigned the reason code `NOT_YET_ELIGIBLE`, `UNABLE_TO_VERIFY` or `CONFLICT` as appropriate in the Recruitment and Selection Log.

---

## 6. Diversity of Experience

The recruitment process should seek candidates with a range of:

- **Project types:** bathroom renovation, internal alterations, subfloor work, decks and verandas, windows and doors, timber deterioration, cladding, water ingress, occupied-home renovation, projects involving escalation to engineer or builder.
- **Years of experience:** varied, not only long-term practitioners.
- **Employment and business context:** employee, subcontractor, sole trader, small business.
- **Geographic location:** varied, not geographically concentrated.
- **Renovation context:** residential, not only commercial or new-build.

No single applicant is required to cover every category. The cohort as a whole should provide useful variation.

---

## 7. Selection Controls

### 7.1 Selection Panel

Candidate selection must not be made by a single person. A selection panel of at least two persons is recommended.

The pilot founder (Dan Sonsie) may participate in recruitment design but should not be the sole decision-maker in candidate selection. Selection decisions must be recorded and reviewable.

### 7.2 Documented Rationale

Every selection and rejection decision must be recorded in the Recruitment and Selection Log (`docs/pilot-recruitment-selection-log-v0.1.md`) with a controlled reason code.

Subjective labels such as "not suitable" must not be used.

### 7.3 Bias Avoidance

See Section 8 — Recruitment Bias Controls.

### 7.4 Independence from Assessors

Where practicable, assessor assignment should occur after candidate selection, so that candidates are not selected based on assessor preference.

---

## 8. Recruitment Bias Controls

The following bias risks must be actively managed:

| Bias Risk | Control |
|---|---|
| Recruiting only reform supporters | Use open and diverse recruitment channels; do not screen for stated position on reform |
| Recruiting only from founder's personal network | Use at least one arm's-length channel (e.g. public EOI, association circulation) |
| Recruiting only highly experienced candidates | Seek variation in experience level; do not require long tenure |
| Recruiting only candidates expected to succeed | Do not screen for expected assessment performance |
| Geographic concentration | Actively seek candidates from diverse locations |
| Business-owner bias | Include employee and subcontractor applicants |
| Self-selection bias | Acknowledge in reporting; do not overclaim representativeness |
| Trade-association bias | Not limited to members of any single association |
| Overrepresentation of one renovation type | Monitor project type distribution during selection |

The pilot does not claim statistical representativeness of the Victorian carpenter population unless the design and sample support that claim.

---

## 9. Oversubscription Handling

If more eligible applicants apply than can be accommodated in the pilot cohort:

- a waitlist is maintained using the Participant Register (`docs/pilot-participant-register-template-v0.1.md`);
- selection from the eligible pool should prioritise cohort diversity (experience type, project category, geographic spread) over any other factor;
- selection must not prioritise applicants based on their expected assessment performance;
- waitlisted applicants are notified of their status and offered a place if a vacancy arises.

---

## 10. Waitlist Handling

Waitlisted applicants:

- are eligible and have been assessed as meeting inclusion criteria;
- have not been rejected;
- are offered a position when a vacancy arises, in order of waitlist priority (determined by selection panel based on cohort balance need);
- may withdraw from the waitlist at any time without consequence.

---

## 11. Withdrawal Before or During Pilot

A candidate may withdraw at any point before or during the pilot. Withdrawal:

- is voluntary and uncoerced;
- does not constitute failure;
- must be recorded in the Participant Register and the Decision Register;
- is managed in accordance with the Withdrawal and Evidence Retention provisions in the Governance Plan (Section 16) and the Privacy and Data Handling Plan.

---

## 12. Replacement of Withdrawn Candidates

Where a candidate withdraws after commencing assessment, a replacement from the waitlist may be selected. Replacement decisions must be recorded and must preserve cohort diversity goals.

---

## 13. Evidence Verification

Before a candidate commences assessment, their eligibility evidence must be verified. Verification may include:

- sighting qualification certificates or apprenticeship records;
- reviewing portfolio materials;
- contacting referees or previous clients where consented;
- reviewing records of previous escalation to engineer, builder, surveyor or licensed trade.

Evidence verification must be conducted by the eligibility reviewer. Verification outcomes must be recorded in the Participant Register.

Where evidence cannot be verified, the reason code `UNABLE_TO_VERIFY` is applied in the Recruitment and Selection Log, and the applicant is excluded unless the issue can be resolved.

---

## 14. Communication with Applicants

All communications with applicants must:

- accurately describe what the pilot is and is not;
- not imply guaranteed selection;
- not imply that participation will confer recognition, registration or authority;
- refer applicants to the Candidate Information Statement (`docs/pilot-candidate-information-statement-v0.1.md`) for full details.

The following notice must appear in all recruitment communications:

> "Submitting an Expression of Interest does not guarantee selection and does not confer recognition of any kind. The pilot tests a capability-assessment framework only. Participation does not create statutory authority, registration, licence or contracting entitlement."

---

## 15. Readiness Gate

No recruitment activity may commence until the readiness checklist (`docs/pilot-governance-readiness-checklist-v0.1.md`) has been completed and a readiness outcome of `READY_TO_RECRUIT_LIMITED` or `READY_WITH_CONDITIONS` has been achieved and documented.

This sprint does not commence live recruitment.
