# Candidate Evidence Requirements — Pilot Version 0.1

**Carpenters for Housing Reform Victoria**
**Status:** DRAFT — pilot evidence requirements for consultation and validation
**Version:** 0.1
**Date:** August 2026

---

## Purpose

This document defines the evidence that candidates must submit for assessment under the Advanced Residential Carpentry Capability Assessment Framework v0.1.

It defines acceptable evidence types, evidence status vocabulary and the metadata required for each evidence item.

---

## 1 — Evidence Status Vocabulary

The following status vocabulary applies to candidate evidence submitted under this framework.

Where the same vocabulary is used elsewhere in the repository, this framework aligns rather than duplicates.

| Code | Meaning |
|---|---|
| `USER_DECLARED` | The candidate has stated this fact or condition. No independent verification has been obtained. |
| `PHOTO_SUPPORTED` | The claim or condition is supported by photographic evidence provided by the candidate. |
| `DOCUMENT_SUPPORTED` | The claim or condition is supported by a document (scope, variation, completion record, permit, invoice or equivalent) provided by the candidate. |
| `EXTERNAL_SOURCE` | The evidence originates from a party other than the candidate — a reference, engineer's report, surveyor's record, pest report or equivalent. |
| `SYSTEM_DERIVED` | The evidence is derived from a system record — registration database, training system, licensing record or equivalent. |
| `PROFESSIONAL_REVIEWED` | The evidence has been reviewed and confirmed by a qualified professional independent of the candidate. |
| `UNKNOWN` | The source or basis of the evidence cannot be determined from available information. |
| `UNABLE_TO_VERIFY` | The evidence cannot be verified from available sources. |

## 2 — Required Evidence Items

The following evidence items are required for assessment. Each item must meet the minimum verification standard indicated.

### 2.1 Eligibility Evidence

| Evidence Item | Minimum Status | Notes |
|---|---|---|
| Recognised carpentry qualification certificate | `SYSTEM_DERIVED` or `DOCUMENT_SUPPORTED` | Original or certified copy |
| Apprenticeship completion record or equivalent | `SYSTEM_DERIVED` or `DOCUMENT_SUPPORTED` | Where applicable |
| Identity document | `DOCUMENT_SUPPORTED` | Government-issued identity |
| Practitioner registration or licensing record | `SYSTEM_DERIVED` | Current |

### 2.2 Portfolio Evidence

Candidates must submit a portfolio of prior projects demonstrating work in existing-building residential renovation contexts.

The portfolio must include evidence across multiple projects and multiple existing-building contexts. It must not consist of a single project only.

For each project included in the portfolio, the candidate must provide:

| Evidence Item | Minimum Status | Capability Domain Relevance |
|---|---|---|
| Initial project scope | `DOCUMENT_SUPPORTED` | Planning, Technical |
| Site photographs — before work | `PHOTO_SUPPORTED` | Technical, Diagnostic |
| Site photographs — during work | `PHOTO_SUPPORTED` | Technical, Structural, Planning |
| Site photographs — after work | `PHOTO_SUPPORTED` | Technical |
| Scope variations or changes | `DOCUMENT_SUPPORTED` | Planning, Professional Judgement |
| Evidence of concealed-condition discovery and response | `PHOTO_SUPPORTED` or `DOCUMENT_SUPPORTED` | Diagnostic, Professional Judgement, Boundary Recognition |
| Evidence of professional referrals (engineer, surveyor, builder, licensed trade) where applicable | `DOCUMENT_SUPPORTED` or `EXTERNAL_SOURCE` | Coordination, Boundary Recognition |
| Consumer communications where relevant | `DOCUMENT_SUPPORTED` | Professional Judgement, Boundary Recognition |
| Completion records | `DOCUMENT_SUPPORTED` | Technical, Planning |

### 2.3 Reference Evidence

Candidates must provide references or equivalent verification from parties with direct knowledge of their work.

| Evidence Item | Minimum Status | Notes |
|---|---|---|
| References from clients, supervising professionals or equivalent | `EXTERNAL_SOURCE` | Minimum number to be determined during pilot design |
| Verification of claimed projects | `EXTERNAL_SOURCE` or `DOCUMENT_SUPPORTED` | Assessors may contact referees |

## 3 — Evidence Item Metadata

Each evidence item submitted must be recorded with the following metadata:

| Metadata Field | Requirement |
|---|---|
| Source | Who or what produced the evidence |
| Date or project context | Date of the evidence or the project to which it relates |
| Candidate role | The candidate's role in the project — lead, subcontracted, employed, supervised |
| Verification status | Status code from the vocabulary in Section 1 |
| Relevance to capability domain | Which of the seven capability domains this evidence supports |
| Privacy and consent status | Whether any privacy or consent obligations apply to the evidence |

---

## 4 — Evidence Quality Principles

### 4.1 Evidence Must Be Traceable

Evidence without a traceable source, date or project context will be treated as `USER_DECLARED` regardless of its apparent form.

### 4.2 Photographs Must Include Context

Photographs submitted without context — no description, no project identification, no date reference — are weak evidence. Photographs with annotation or accompanying documentation are stronger evidence.

### 4.3 Candidate-Declared Evidence Is Acceptable but Carries Less Weight

`USER_DECLARED` evidence is acceptable for initial assessment purposes. Assessors must note where significant claims rest on `USER_DECLARED` evidence alone and reflect this in their scoring rationale.

### 4.4 Missing Evidence Must Not Be Treated as Evidence of Competence

Where a candidate cannot produce evidence of a particular type of work or decision, assessors must not infer that the absence of the evidence means no problem arose. The absence of evidence is a gap, not a positive indicator.

### 4.5 Privacy and Consent

Candidates are responsible for ensuring that evidence submitted does not breach the privacy of consumers, other parties or third-party commercial arrangements without appropriate consent or redaction.

The assessment body must inform candidates of privacy obligations before evidence is submitted.

## 5 — Evidence Sufficiency for Each Domain

The following provides guidance on the minimum evidence expected per capability domain. This guidance is illustrative, not exhaustive.

| Domain | Minimum Evidence Indicators |
|---|---|
| Technical Capability | Project photos showing construction stages; scope documents; description of materials and methods used |
| Diagnostic Capability | Records of pre-work condition assessment; evidence of concealed-condition discovery and response |
| Structural Understanding | Evidence of structural element identification; referral records where engineering input was required |
| Planning Capability | Initial scope and variation documents; evidence of sequencing decisions |
| Coordination Capability | Evidence of engagement with other trades or professionals; referral records |
| Professional Judgement | Complex project records showing multiple decision points; consumer communication records |
| Boundary Recognition | Evidence of escalation decisions; referral records; stop-work records where applicable |
