# RRC Pilot Evidence Schedule
**Carpenters for Housing Reform Victoria**
Version: 1.0 — Working Draft
Date: July 2026
Status: WORKING DRAFT — Minimum evidence schedule for pilot projects. Requires government and regulatory development.

## Purpose

The purpose of this schedule is to identify the minimum evidence that should be collected during an RRC pilot so that consumer protection, practitioner conduct, scope boundaries and escalation decisions can be evaluated in a disciplined way.

This schedule is a working draft, not an operative legal standard. Final evidence settings, record formats and retention obligations require government, regulator and legal development.

## Evidence Categories

The pilot should distinguish four practical categories of evidence.

**Mandatory evidence** is the core record set required for every pilot project.

**Conditionally required evidence** becomes mandatory when a trigger occurs.

**Optional supporting evidence** can strengthen the record where available.

Two evidence status markers should also be used. **Evidence status: Unavailable** should be used where a record type could not reasonably be obtained. **Evidence status: Unable to verify** should be used where the practitioner could not independently confirm an item.

## Optional Supporting Evidence

Optional supporting evidence may include:

- additional photographs;
- measurements and sketches;
- material records;
- subcontractor records; and
- correspondence with homeowner.

These items may improve auditability and dispute resolution. However, they should supplement, not replace, the mandatory record set.

## Evidence Limitations

Evidence collection improves visibility, but it has limits.

Photographs alone do not establish every technical property. They may show deterioration, movement or exposure, but they do not reliably prove structural adequacy, legal status or cause.

Photographs do not prove legal compliance.

Evidence completeness depends on practitioner diligence.

Some concealed conditions cannot be photographed or measured until disturbed.

Evidence requirements may change through pilot design. This working draft should therefore be treated as a base schedule for development, not a final code.

## Record Retention

Records should be retained for a minimum of 7 years (**PROPOSED — REQUIRES GOVERNMENT DECISION**), remain accessible for audit, and be maintained in the format agreed with the pilot authority.
