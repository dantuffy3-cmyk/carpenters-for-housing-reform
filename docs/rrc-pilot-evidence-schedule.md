# RRC Pilot Evidence Schedule
**Carpenters for Housing Reform Victoria**
Version: 1.1 — Working Draft
Date: August 2026
Status: WORKING DRAFT — Minimum evidence schedule for pilot projects. Requires government and regulatory development.

## Purpose

This schedule identifies the minimum evidence needed for a pilot that tests capability rather than new statutory authority.

Participants remain within their existing lawful authority. The record system is a pilot governance requirement, not a statutory building manual.

## Persistent Pilot Work and Evidence Record

Every pilot project should maintain a persistent record containing at least the following fields:

| Required Field | Purpose |
|---|---|
| Practitioner identity | Identify the assessed participant responsible for the record |
| Assessed scope | Record the capability boundary under which the participant was assessed |
| Pre-existing conditions | Record the known condition of the site before work |
| Work undertaken | Record what work was actually carried out within scope |
| Evidence captured | Record the documentary and photographic evidence collected |
| Variations | Record any scope changes or revised work sequence |
| Concealed conditions | Record discoveries made during the work |
| Escalation events | Record stop-work, pause and escalation decisions |
| Professional referrals or handoffs | Record builder, engineer, surveyor or licensed-trade involvement |
| Completion status | Record whether work completed, paused, transferred or closed with issues outstanding |

## Mandatory Evidence

| Evidence Item | When Required | Notes |
|---|---|---|
| Qualification verification | Before admission | Confirms qualified-carpenter entry |
| Verified practical experience record | Before admission | Confirms experience as evidence of exposure and eligibility |
| Portfolio evidence | Before admission | Supports capability assessment |
| Assessment outcome record | At admission decision | Records independent assessment and any conditions |
| Consumer disclosure acknowledgement | Before work begins | Records role explanation and pilot limitations |
| Initial condition record | Before disturbance starts | Includes pre-existing conditions and baseline evidence |
| Work and decision notes | During the project | Captures observations, decisions and sequencing |
| Concealed-condition record | When applicable | Mandatory when a hidden condition is discovered |
| Variation or scope-change record | When applicable | Mandatory when scope changes are considered |
| Escalation or handoff record | When applicable | Mandatory when a builder, engineer, surveyor or licensed trade becomes involved |
| Completion record | At close-out | Records final status and any unresolved matters |

## Consumer Role-Comprehension Evidence

The pilot should also retain evidence showing whether consumers understood:

- what the practitioner could do;
- what remained outside scope;
- when a builder was required; and
- when engineering, permits or licensed trades were required.

## Record Limitations

- The pilot record is not a statutory building manual.
- The record should not be represented as proof of legal compliance beyond what the underlying evidence can establish.
- Building-manual requirements for new Class 1b, 2 and 3 buildings must not be represented as Class 1a renovation requirements.
