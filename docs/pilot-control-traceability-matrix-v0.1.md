## Gap visibility notes

Potential governance gaps remain visible where unresolved questions affect:
- legal review scope and pilot permissibility;
- assessor composition and validated consistency thresholds;
- retention and recording settings;
- observer access terms; and
- the government vehicle question.

This matrix is therefore a control map, not a claim that every control is fully resolved.
