**Version:** 0.1  
**Status:** Internal governance foundation

## Purpose

Version 0.1 establishes the governed foundation for claims, sources, unresolved questions, wording controls and practitioner-case placeholders used across the Carpenters for Housing Reform Victoria initiative.

This version is an internal governance foundation only. It does not yet represent a completed audit of every live website claim.

## Evidence hierarchy

1. Current primary sources and direct documentary evidence
2. Professional or institutional materials with identifiable accountability
3. Secondary analysis
4. Practitioner evidence and contextual material
5. Discovery-only or unverified material

Practitioner evidence can inform the work, but it does not replace current primary sources where a claim is to be represented as verified.

## Claim lifecycle

1. Record the claim in controlled form.
2. Classify the claim type and category.
3. Record evidence status, information basis and limitations.
4. Link source IDs and practitioner-case IDs rather than embedding unsupported narrative.
5. Apply review status and publication status separately.
6. Supersede outdated sources and claims rather than leaving them to drift.
7. Re-review when triggers, dates or new evidence require it.

## Publication controls

The full governed register remains internal. A future public register may surface only approved publication-safe records.

Public release must exclude personal information, sensitive project information, legally privileged material, unverified private allegations, internal strategy, draft legal interpretations and confidential stakeholder correspondence.

## Source currency rules

- No claim is to be represented as verified unless supported by a current primary source or direct documentary evidence.
- Third-party AI output is not evidence.
- Outdated sources are to be superseded rather than silently retained.
- Unsupported or unresolved matters remain visibly unresolved.

## Distinction between evidence status and publication status

Evidence status records how well a claim is supported.

Publication status records whether a claim may be released publicly, released only in filtered form, or kept internal.

A claim may be well-supported and still remain internal-only. A claim may also have approved public wording while still carrying qualifications or limitations.
