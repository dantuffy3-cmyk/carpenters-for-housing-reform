# Interstate comparisons

NSW has carpentry contractor licences and separate qualified-supervisor certificates for defined work; its published scope excludes whole garages, sheds and carports. Queensland has a carpentry licence class and contractor-type licences allowing direct contracts within licensed scope, with business and financial requirements. Neither scheme automatically transfers its permissions to Victoria.
