# Stakeholder Claims Register

**Carpenters for Housing Reform Victoria**
Last reviewed: July 2026

---

## Purpose

This register records the important public claims made by Carpenters for Housing Reform Victoria, identifies the evidence supporting each claim, and documents approved and prohibited wording. It is maintained to ensure the initiative's credibility, prevent overstatement and support governance review.

Claims are classified by evidence status:

- **Verified** — supported by legislation, regulation, official guidance, government data, recognised standards or published research
- **Practitioner evidence** — based on documented practitioner observation or experience
- **Policy analysis** — reasoned evaluation of options, risks and benefits
- **Scenario analysis** — hypothetical or future-facing material used to test the proposal
- **TODO** — authoritative evidence still required
