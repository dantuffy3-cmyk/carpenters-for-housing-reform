# Stakeholder Claims Register

**Carpenters for Housing Reform Victoria**
Last reviewed: July 2026

---

## Purpose

This register records the important public claims made by Carpenters for Housing Reform Victoria, identifies the evidence supporting each claim, and documents approved and prohibited wording. It is maintained to ensure the initiative's credibility, prevent overstatement and support governance review.

Claims are classified by evidence status:

- **Verified** — supported by legislation, regulation, official guidance, government data, recognised standards or published research
- **Practitioner evidence** — based on documented practitioner observation or experience
- **Policy analysis** — reasoned evaluation of options, risks and benefits
- **Scenario analysis** — hypothetical or future-facing material used to test the proposal
- **TODO** — authoritative evidence still required

## Claim 3 — Alignment with Victoria's 2026 Building Reform Framework

**Claim:** The proposed RRC pathway is designed to support the direction of Victoria's 2026 building reforms and the Building and Plumbing Commission framework.

**Evidence source:** Victoria's building reform announcements (publicly available); Building and Plumbing Commission establishment information.

**Evidence status:** Policy analysis — the alignment is the initiative's interpretation of its proposal's compatibility with public reform directions. It is not a statement that the government has assessed or accepted that alignment.

**Approved wording:** "designed to support that direction"; "consistent with the reform objective of improved consumer protection"; "intended to complement the BPC framework"

**Prohibited overstatement:**
- Do not state that government has assessed the RRC proposal as aligned with the 2026 reforms
- Do not imply government endorsement of the proposed alignment
- Do not claim BPC has reviewed or approved the proposal

**Date reviewed:** July 2026

## Claim 4 — Consumer Benefits

**Claim:** A functioning RRC pathway would improve consumer clarity about renovation responsibility and provide better consumer protection.

**Evidence source:** Policy analysis — based on the proposed pathway's design features (published scope, consumer disclosure, complaints pathway, mandatory handoff triggers).

**Evidence status:** Policy analysis. Consumer benefit claims are conditional on the pathway being designed and implemented as proposed. They are not verified empirical outcomes.

**Approved wording:** "intended to improve consumer clarity"; "designed to provide better consumer protection"; "would give consumers clearer guidance on"; "consumer benefit depends on effective implementation"

**Prohibited overstatement:**
- Do not claim consumer benefits as established facts
- Do not cite consumer harm statistics unless sourced from authoritative published data
- Do not claim the pathway will eliminate consumer detriment in renovation

**Date reviewed:** July 2026
