## Important

This document records the outcomes of an assessor calibration session. It is an internal governance record.

Calibration results are not candidate assessment outcomes. They do not confer practitioner authority or recognition on any participant.

Readiness recommendations in this document are calibration-readiness outcomes only. They do not constitute approval to commence statutory assessment, issue credentials or make regulatory determinations.
