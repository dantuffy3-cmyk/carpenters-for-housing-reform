**Status:** WORKING DRAFT

---

## Scope

This declaration applies to all persons serving in the following roles in the ARC capability-assessment pilot:

- assessors;
- second assessors;
- calibration chair;
- technical or governance reviewers;
- governance participants;
- pilot coordinator;
- pilot sponsor / initiative lead.

All persons in these roles must complete this declaration before commencing their role and before each candidate assignment.

---

## Important Notice

A conflict of interest does not automatically disqualify a person from participating. The purpose of this declaration is to ensure that conflicts are identified, considered and managed — not to exclude all persons with any connection to the field.

The outcome of the declaration determines how the conflict is managed, not whether involvement is possible.
