## Part 2 — Request Objective

The objective of this information request is to understand the current high-level assessment architecture for DB-L Carpentry, so that stakeholder analysis does not incorrectly infer capability gaps from the absence of publicly available information.

This request is **not** seeking:

- examination questions;
- question banks;
- live assessment scenarios;
- candidate records or candidate performance data;
- secure marking guides;
- protected assessment material of any kind;
- personal information.

The purpose is solely to ensure that the absence of public information is not treated as evidence that a capability is not assessed.

Dear Building and Plumbing Commission,

Carpenters for Housing Reform Victoria is preparing evidence-based stakeholder feedback in relation to Victoria's forthcoming carpentry registration and licensing consultation.

As part of that work, we are seeking to ensure that our analysis accurately reflects the current Domestic Builder Limited — Carpentry assessment process.

We are not seeking examination questions, candidate information or any material that could compromise assessment integrity.

If available, we would appreciate high-level information or existing published material describing:

1. the broad competencies, knowledge areas and professional judgement assessed for DB-L Carpentry;
2. the assessment methods used, including the role of qualifications, practical experience, Technical Referee evidence and any examination or other assessment process;
3. how professional judgement and recognition of scope limitations, referral obligations or escalation obligations are treated within the assessment architecture;
4. whether existing-building, renovation, alteration or addition work is specifically addressed within the assessment architecture;
5. the general approach used to support consistency or calibration in assessment decisions, where this can be disclosed without compromising assessment integrity.

A high-level summary, reference to existing published material or a briefing clarification would be sufficient.

The purpose of this request is to avoid incorrectly treating the absence of publicly available information as evidence that a capability is not assessed.

Thank you for any guidance you are able to provide.

Kind regards,

Dan Sonsie
Carpenters for Housing Reform Victoria

## Part 4 — Terminology Control

When sending this request, **do not ask** BPC whether DB-L assesses the ARC seven capability domains.

Instead, ask BPC to describe, in their own terms:

- the competency categories they apply;
- the knowledge areas they assess;
- the judgement requirements they test;
- the scope-limitation and boundary behaviours they address;
- the assessment methods they use.

**BPC terminology must be captured first.**

Any mapping from BPC-described competencies to the seven ARC domains must occur internally afterwards. The ARC taxonomy must not be imposed on BPC or implied in the question.
