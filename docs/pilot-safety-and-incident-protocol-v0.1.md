# Pilot Safety and Incident Protocol — v0.1

**Status:** WORKING DRAFT

**Prepared by:** Carpenters for Housing Reform Victoria
**Version:** 0.1
**Date:** August 2026

---

## Purpose

This protocol defines how safety concerns and incidents are identified, escalated and managed during the ARC capability-assessment pilot.

Safety takes priority over completion of any pilot activity. See Governance Principle 12.

---

## Assessment Safety Baseline

Version 0.1 of the ARC assessment does not require or involve:

- live structural building work;
- deliberate exposure to physical hazards;
- entry into unsafe structures;
- use of power tools or heavy equipment.

If any proposed assessment activity requires any of the above, that activity must be reviewed and redesigned before it proceeds.

If a safety issue emerges unexpectedly during assessment, the activity must stop immediately.

---

## Incident Categories

| Code | Category | Description |
|---|---|---|
| MEDICAL | Medical emergency | A participant requires immediate medical attention |
| DISTRESS | Participant distress | A participant appears to be in significant psychological distress |
| AGGRESSION | Aggressive conduct | A participant, assessor or observer behaves aggressively toward another person |
| PRIVACY | Privacy incident | Personal data is accessed, disclosed or handled in breach of the Privacy and Data Handling Plan |
| EVIDENCE_INTEGRITY | Evidence integrity incident | Assessment evidence appears to have been altered, fabricated or compromised |
| FABRICATION | Suspected fabrication | A participant's evidence or representations appear to be materially false |
| CONFLICT_DISCOVERED | Conflict discovered mid-process | A previously undisclosed conflict is identified during assessment |
| LEGAL_REGULATORY | Legal or regulatory concern | A concern arises about the lawfulness or regulatory compliance of a pilot activity |
| SAFETY_ENVIRONMENT | Environmental safety concern | A physical environment risk is identified in an assessment setting |
| OTHER | Other incident | Any other incident not covered by the categories above |

---

## Immediate Response

For any incident, the immediate response is:

1. **Stop** the affected activity.
2. **Ensure** the immediate safety and wellbeing of all persons present.
3. **Notify** the pilot coordinator as soon as practicable.
4. **Record** the incident using the Incident Record form below.
5. **Do not continue** the activity until the incident has been reviewed and an outcome has been determined.

For a MEDICAL emergency, call emergency services (000) first. Then notify the pilot coordinator.

---

## Incident Outcomes

| Outcome Code | Meaning |
|---|---|
| CONTINUE_WITH_CONTROLS | Activity may continue after controls are put in place |
| PAUSE_PARTICIPANT | This participant's involvement is paused pending review |
| PAUSE_ACTIVITY | This assessment activity is paused for all participants pending review |
| ESCALATE_FOR_REVIEW | The incident must be reviewed before any further activity |
| STOP_PARTICIPATION | This participant's involvement ends |
| STOP_PILOT_COMPONENT | A component of the pilot is stopped pending redesign or governance review |

Incident outcomes are determined by the pilot coordinator in consultation with the pilot sponsor where required. All outcomes must be recorded in the Pilot Decision Register.

## Specific Incident Guidance

### Medical Emergency

1. Call emergency services (000).
2. Ensure the person's immediate safety.
3. Notify the pilot coordinator.
4. Do not continue assessment activity.
5. Record the incident.
6. Outcome: determined after the immediate emergency is resolved.

### Participant Distress

1. Pause the assessment activity.
2. Provide the participant with space and support.
3. Notify the pilot coordinator.
4. Do not pressure the participant to continue.
5. Record the incident.
6. Outcome: PAUSE_PARTICIPANT pending participant's own decision about continuation.

### Aggressive Conduct

1. Stop the activity.
2. Ensure the safety of all persons.
3. Notify the pilot coordinator immediately.
4. Record the incident.
5. Outcome: at minimum PAUSE_PARTICIPANT; may escalate to STOP_PARTICIPATION.

### Privacy Incident

1. Stop any activity that is contributing to the breach.
2. Notify the evidence custodian and pilot coordinator.
3. Record the incident under category PRIVACY.
4. Notify affected participants as soon as practicable.
5. Document remediation in the Pilot Decision Register.
6. Outcome: PAUSE_ACTIVITY or ESCALATE_FOR_REVIEW pending scope assessment.

### Evidence Integrity or Suspected Fabrication

1. Pause the relevant assessment.
2. Do not confront the participant directly until reviewed.
3. Notify the pilot coordinator and pilot sponsor.
4. Preserve all records in their current state.
5. Record the incident.
6. Outcome: PAUSE_PARTICIPANT or ESCALATE_FOR_REVIEW pending investigation.

### Conflict Discovered Mid-Process

1. Pause the affected assessment activity.
2. Notify the pilot coordinator.
3. Record the conflict in the Conflict of Interest Declaration for the relevant person.
4. Record the incident in the Pilot Decision Register.
5. Determine recusal and reassignment if required.
6. Outcome: determined by conflict declaration review.

### Legal or Regulatory Concern

1. Stop the affected activity.
2. Notify the pilot coordinator and pilot sponsor.
3. Record the concern in the Pilot Decision Register under category LEGAL_REGULATORY.
4. Seek legal or governance advice before resuming.
5. Outcome: PAUSE_ACTIVITY or ESCALATE_FOR_REVIEW.

## Withdrawal After an Incident

Participants are not required to continue after an incident. Withdrawal following an incident must not be characterised as failure. See Governance Principle 13 and the Withdrawal provisions in the Governance Plan.
