# Stakeholder Outreach Register
**Carpenters for Housing Reform Victoria**
Version: 1.0
Date: July 2026
Status: WORKING DOCUMENT — For internal use. Update after each contact or meeting.

## Purpose
Track all outreach to stakeholders, ensure follow-up discipline, and record feedback and meeting outcomes.

## Register Fields

- **Stakeholder:** Individual name or "to be identified".
- **Organisation:** Organisation name.
- **Category:** Stakeholder category.
- **Relevance:** Why this stakeholder matters to the pilot.
- **Contact status:** Not contacted / Approached / Awaiting response / Responded / Declined / Meeting scheduled / Meeting held.
- **Material sent:** List of documents sent.
- **Date contacted:** ISO date or blank.
- **Response:** Summary of response received.
- **Concerns raised:** Key concerns.
- **Follow-up action:** Required action.
- **Follow-up date:** Date.
- **Meeting requested:** Yes/No.
- **Meeting held:** Yes/No.
- **Outcome:** Outcome of meeting or contact.
- **Next step:** Agreed next step.
- **Evidence or correspondence reference:** File reference.
