# ADR-003: Governed Claims and Evidence Register

## Status

Accepted

## Context

The reform now contains website content, policy propositions, practitioner experience, official sources and third-party analysis. Without a governed register, unsupported or outdated claims could enter public material.

## Decision

Claims, sources, unresolved questions, wording controls and practitioner cases will be maintained as separate governed records.

The complete register will remain internal.

Only approved and publication-safe records may be surfaced publicly.

## Consequences

### Positive

- traceability;
- source currency;
- wording consistency;
- separation of fact and interpretation;
- controlled public disclosure;
- better government credibility;
- resistance to AI hallucination and outdated information.

### Trade-offs

- additional maintenance;
- claims require review before publication;
- public content may move more slowly;
- unresolved matters must remain visibly unresolved.
