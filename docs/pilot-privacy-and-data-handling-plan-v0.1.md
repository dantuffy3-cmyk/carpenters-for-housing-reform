# Pilot Privacy and Data Handling Plan — v0.1

**Status:** WORKING DRAFT

**Prepared by:** Carpenters for Housing Reform Victoria
**Version:** 0.1
**Date:** August 2026

---

## Important Notice

This plan is a working draft. Final privacy obligations and retention requirements require legal and governance review before live pilot commencement. This document does not constitute legal advice and does not make absolute legal compliance claims.

---

## 1. Purpose

This plan defines how participant data is collected, stored, accessed, used, retained and handled during and after the ARC capability-assessment pilot.

The plan applies to all pilot participants including candidate carpenters, assessors, reviewers, consumer participants and observers.

---

## 2. Data Collected

The following categories of data may be collected during the pilot:

| Data Category | Purpose | Examples |
|---|---|---|
| Identity information | Participant identification and communication | Name, contact details, location |
| Qualification and experience records | Eligibility screening and assessment | Certificates, apprenticeship records, experience descriptions |
| Portfolio evidence | Capability assessment | Project photographs, written descriptions, records |
| Assessment records | Recording assessment activities and outcomes | Interview notes, scenario responses, assessor observations |
| Conflict declarations | Governance and independence management | Declared relationships, advocacy positions |
| Consent records | Legal and governance record | Signed consent forms |
| Recruitment records | Audit and accountability | EOI submissions, eligibility decisions, selection rationale |
| Incident records | Safety and governance | Incident reports, follow-up actions |
| Withdrawal records | Governance | Date of withdrawal, reason (controlled code) |
| Observer records | Governance | Observer identity, access granted, confidentiality signed |

---

## 3. Collection Purpose

Data is collected for the following pilot purposes only:

- eligibility screening and candidate selection;
- capability assessment;
- assessor calibration and quality review;
- governance, audit and accountability;
- aggregate and anonymised pilot reporting;
- complaints and review processing.

Data is not collected for commercial, marketing or non-pilot purposes.

---

## 3A. Third-Party Information in Portfolio Evidence

Portfolio evidence submitted by candidate carpenters may contain information belonging to third parties — including homeowners, clients, engineers, builders and other parties involved in renovation projects. This information may include:

- property addresses;
- client or homeowner names;
- project photographs showing private residential premises;
- contracts, invoices or correspondence;
- engineering or specialist reports;
- permit documents or records;
- communications with other parties.

The candidate may not have unilateral authority to disclose this information to the pilot.

Before assessment commences, candidates should be advised to:

- consider whether third-party identifying information can be de-identified or redacted without affecting the evidence quality;
- consider whether any material they propose to submit is subject to confidentiality obligations;
- seek appropriate permission where submission of identifiable third-party material is required.

The pilot does not automatically require candidates to submit identifiable third-party information. Assessors must not request more third-party information than is necessary to assess the candidate's demonstrated capability.

This area requires legal and governance review before live pilot commencement. The appropriate approach to third-party information in candidate portfolio evidence is an unresolved question and must not be resolved by assumption.

---

## 4. Minimum Necessary Collection

Only the minimum data necessary for each purpose is collected. Data collection fields may be reviewed and reduced before live pilot commencement.

---

## 5. Participant Identifiers

Participants are assigned a Participant ID before assessment commences. Where possible, assessment records reference the Participant ID rather than full name.

The link between Participant ID and identity is held by the evidence custodian and is not distributed in assessment working documents.

---

## 6. Anonymisation and Pseudonymisation

Aggregate pilot reports use anonymised or pseudonymised data. No individual is identified in published outputs unless they have separately provided written publication consent (Part B of the Participant Consent Form).

Anonymisation or pseudonymisation methodology will be defined before live pilot commencement.

---

## 7. Storage

Pilot data is stored:

- in secured digital storage with access controls;
- separately from any public-facing systems;
- using access limited to authorised pilot personnel.

Storage platform, encryption standards and access control mechanisms will be defined before live pilot commencement.

---

## 8. Access Controls

Access to identifiable participant data is limited to:

- the evidence custodian;
- the pilot coordinator (for consent and logistics records);
- assigned assessors (for assessment records of their assigned candidates only);
- the pilot sponsor, for governance and audit purposes.

Observers, industry bodies and other third parties do not have access to identifiable participant data unless separately governed.

---

## 9. Retention

Pilot records must be retained for a period sufficient to support audit, review and complaint processes.

The required retention period is an unresolved question. See Unresolved Question [Q-023].

Records must not be retained longer than necessary for the stated purposes.

---

## 10. Destruction

At the end of the retention period, identifiable records are destroyed using a documented and secure process.

Anonymised aggregate findings may be retained as part of the pilot's evidence base.

---

## 11. Correction

Participants may request correction of their personal information by contacting the pilot coordinator. Correction requests are recorded and actioned within a reasonable timeframe.

---

## 12. Withdrawal

Where a participant withdraws, their future participation ceases and their identifiable records are handled according to their consent record.

Evidence already incorporated into anonymised aggregate analysis may not always be technically removable. This limitation will be disclosed to participants in the Candidate Information Statement and Consent Form.

---

## 13. Published Outputs

Published pilot outputs use anonymised or pseudonymised data unless separate publication consent has been obtained.

Before any named material is published, the participant's publication consent (Part B) must be verified.

No participant data is shared with media or public platforms without explicit named consent.

---

## 14. Third-Party Sharing

Identifiable participant data is not shared with third parties (including government, industry bodies or insurers) without:

- specific consent from the participant; or
- a documented governance decision where legal or safety obligations require it.

Third-party sharing decisions must be recorded in the Pilot Decision Register.

---

## 15. Breach and Incident Handling

If a data breach or privacy incident is identified:

1. The evidence custodian and pilot coordinator are notified immediately.
2. The incident is recorded using the Safety and Incident Protocol (`docs/pilot-safety-and-incident-protocol-v0.1.md`) with category: PRIVACY.
3. Affected participants are notified as soon as practicable.
4. Remediation steps are documented in the Pilot Decision Register.
5. Whether a statutory notification obligation exists requires legal advice and will be addressed through governance review before live pilot commencement.

---

## 16. Observer Data

Observer identity and access records are held separately from participant assessment records. Observers who are required to sign confidentiality agreements are recorded in the Observer Protocol.

---

## 17. Unresolved Questions

The following privacy-related questions remain open:

- Q-023: Required retention period for pilot records
- Q-024: Whether audio or video recording is used and associated obligations
- Q-027: Scope and nature of legal review required before live pilot commencement
- Unresolved — third-party information in candidate portfolio evidence: the appropriate framework for candidate disclosure of homeowner or client information requires legal review and cannot be resolved by internal governance assumption alone.
