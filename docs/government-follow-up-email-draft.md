Advanced Residential Carpentry — alignment with practitioner registration reform

## Draft Email

---

Dear [Name],

Thank you for [the acknowledgement of our earlier correspondence / our recent meeting / the opportunity to submit materials] regarding Victoria's building reform program.

I am writing on behalf of Carpenters for Housing Reform Victoria to follow up on a matter that we believe aligns with the current direction of Victoria's enhanced practitioner registration reforms.

**What we have done since last contact**

We have refined our proposal in response to the Victorian Government's registration reform direction. We are no longer asking government to predetermine a standalone registration class. Instead, our ask is more specific: we are asking whether advanced residential renovation carpentry could be considered as a candidate practitioner function — or assessment stream — within Victoria's enhanced registration framework.

We have developed a capability framework describing the distinct competence required for advanced residential renovation work, a proposed assessment architecture, and a draft pilot design that grants no additional statutory authority to participants.

**Our current ask**

We would welcome the opportunity to ask two questions:

1. Could advanced residential renovation carpentry be considered as a candidate practitioner function within Victoria's enhanced registration framework?

2. Would the Building Policy Reform Team be open to a brief discussion about a controlled capability-assessment pilot — one that tests whether the proposed framework works before government determines any statutory vehicle?

**What we are offering**

I am happy to provide the short Government Registration Alignment Brief, which maps our proposed capability model against the Government's current registration reform principles and sets out four possible implementation vehicles without recommending one over another.

I am also happy to discuss the pilot design, which is structured to generate evidence rather than to assume outcomes.

**What we are not asking**

We are not asking government to endorse this initiative, adopt the Registered Residential Carpenter model, or make any regulatory commitment at this stage. We are asking only that advanced residential renovation carpentry be considered as a capability question within the current reform process.

I would appreciate the opportunity to discuss this further at your convenience.

Yours sincerely,

Dan Sonsie
Founder, Carpenters for Housing Reform Victoria
Evidence custodian, Advanced Residential Carpentry capability framework
