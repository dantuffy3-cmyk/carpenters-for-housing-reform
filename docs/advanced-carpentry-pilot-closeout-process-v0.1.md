# Advanced Residential Carpentry Pilot Closeout Process — v0.1

**Status:** WORKING DRAFT
**Prepared by:** Carpenters for Housing Reform Victoria
**Version:** 0.1
**Date:** August 2026

---

## Purpose

This process defines how the pilot is closed in a governed way after active delivery, pause, redesign or termination.

Closeout recommendations are pilot governance recommendations only. They do not confer statutory authority and do not determine any government decision.

---

## Pilot completion criteria

Pilot closeout may begin when one of the following applies:

- the planned pilot stage or cohort has been completed;
- the pilot has been paused and will not resume until after a governed review;
- the pilot has been narrowed, redesigned or stopped;
- the pilot has generated enough aggregate evidence for the current evaluation phase; or
- the pilot sponsor records that continuation cannot currently produce reliable further evidence.

Before closeout, the pilot coordinator confirms that:
- candidate outcomes have been recorded or formally voided where required;
- complaints and incident status are known;
- record custody is current;
- unresolved questions affecting interpretation are listed; and
- publication controls remain intact.

---

## Closeout steps

### 1. Confirm closeout trigger
- Identify whether closeout follows completion, pause, redesign or stop.
- Record the closeout trigger in the Pilot Decision Register.

### 2. Capture unresolved issues
- Consolidate unresolved issues from assessment, calibration, complaints, privacy, legal, observer and publication controls.
- Distinguish unresolved issue from pilot failure.

### 3. Participant notification
- Notify affected participants that the pilot stage is closing.
- Explain whether any further action, review right or follow-up communication remains.
- Do not characterise withdrawal or non-completion as failure unless the controlled outcome record requires that description.

### 4. Data closure
- Confirm all participant records are filed against Participant IDs.
- Confirm access controls remain active.
- Confirm identifiable and publication-controlled material is separated appropriately.
- Apply the Privacy and Data Handling Plan to any withdrawal or correction requests.

### 5. Assessor debrief
- Debrief assessors on administration issues, rubric ambiguity, variance themes and record burdens.
- Record any recommended framework clarifications separately from candidate outcomes.

### 6. Calibration review
- Review whether live pilot use revealed divergence requiring recalibration or framework revision.
- Record whether the current panel would be considered ready for any further phase.

### 7. Complaint closure
- Confirm each complaint is closed, escalated or left open with reason.
- Distinguish process findings from competence findings.

### 8. Incident closure
- Confirm each incident has a final recorded outcome.
- Record any continuing control obligations before any later re-commencement.

### 9. Aggregate findings
- Prepare a de-identified aggregate findings set covering outcomes, evidence quality, variance, consumer comprehension and limitations.
- Retain adverse and null findings.

### 10. Publication review
- Check whether any proposed publication uses only de-identified or separately consented material.
- Confirm that observer participation is not represented as endorsement.

### 11. Government reporting
- Prepare any government-facing closeout summary as aggregate evidence and limitation reporting only.
- Do not recommend a statutory vehicle merely because the pilot closed successfully.

### 12. Decision register closure
- Record final closeout decision, outstanding actions and review date if any.

### 13. Lessons learned
- Record what improved, what failed, what remained unclear and what should change before any future phase.

### 14. Future recommendation
- Issue one controlled closeout recommendation from the list below.

## Controlled closeout records

| Record | Lead owner | Minimum closeout action |
|---|---|---|
| Participant register | Pilot Coordinator | Confirm final participant status |
| Assessment records | Evidence Custodian | Confirm complete custody and access controls |
| Calibration results | Calibration Chair | Confirm readiness / recalibration position |
| Complaint records | Pilot Coordinator | Confirm open/closed/escalated status |
| Incident records | Pilot Coordinator | Confirm final outcome code |
| Decision register | Pilot Sponsor / Pilot Coordinator | Record closeout trigger and recommendation |
| Aggregate evaluation record | Pilot evaluation team | Confirm limitation statement included |
| Government summary | Initiative Lead / policy team | Confirm no automatic recognition claim |

## Possible closeout recommendations

- `PROCEED_TO_POLICY_EVALUATION`
- `FURTHER_PILOT_REQUIRED`
- `FRAMEWORK_REVISION_REQUIRED`
- `LEGAL_OR_REGULATORY_REVIEW_REQUIRED`
- `INSUFFICIENT_EVIDENCE`
- `NO_FURTHER_ACTION_RECOMMENDED`
- `UNABLE_TO_FORM_RELIABLE_POSITION`

These are pilot closeout recommendations only. They do not confer authority.

---

## Closeout decision considerations

When selecting a recommendation, consider:
- whether the practitioner function remained distinguishable;
- whether the capability domains remained observable;
- whether outcomes were reviewable and auditable;
- whether assessor variance remained within a defensible range;
- whether critical boundaries were recognised consistently;
- whether privacy, consent and observer controls remained effective; and
- whether the evidence set supports further policy evaluation at all.

Pilot closeout must not claim that a statutory category is justified merely because the pilot was completed.
