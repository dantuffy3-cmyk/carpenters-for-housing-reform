# Pilot Governance Readiness Checklist — v0.1

**Status:** WORKING DRAFT

**Prepared by:** Carpenters for Housing Reform Victoria
**Version:** 0.1
**Date:** August 2026

---

## Purpose

This checklist must be completed and a readiness outcome recorded before any live candidate recruitment commences.

**No recruitment of actual pilot participants may begin until this checklist is completed and a readiness outcome of `READY_TO_RECRUIT_LIMITED` or `READY_WITH_CONDITIONS` is recorded and approved by the pilot sponsor.**

---

## Readiness Outcomes

| Outcome Code | Meaning |
|---|---|
| READY_TO_RECRUIT_LIMITED | All critical items are complete. Recruitment may commence on a limited or controlled basis. |
| READY_WITH_CONDITIONS | Most critical items complete. Identified conditions must be met before or during recruitment. |
| NOT_READY_GOVERNANCE_GAP | One or more governance items are incomplete. Recruitment must not commence. |
| NOT_READY_LEGAL_OR_PRIVACY_REVIEW | Legal or privacy review has not been completed to the required standard. Recruitment must not commence. |
| PILOT_REDESIGN_REQUIRED | A fundamental aspect of the pilot design requires revision before readiness can be assessed. |
| UNABLE_TO_FORM_RELIABLE_POSITION | Insufficient information to determine readiness. |

These are pilot-governance readiness states only. They do not constitute a statutory determination.
