# Advanced Residential Carpentry Pilot Master Operating Model — v0.1

**Status:** WORKING DRAFT — master pilot operating architecture for consultation and validation.
**Prepared by:** Carpenters for Housing Reform Victoria
**Version:** 0.1
**Date:** August 2026

---

## Important Notice

This master model is an **integration document**. It brings the current pilot governance, assessment, calibration, evidence and review architecture into a single governed operating map.

It does **not** replace the detailed assessment, calibration, governance, recruitment, privacy, complaints, safety or evidence documents.

Where any conflict exists, the relevant detailed governed source document controls.

Participation in the pilot remains voluntary. Assessment outcomes remain capability evidence only. They do not confer statutory authority, registration, licence, builder authority, engineering authority, surveyor authority, licensed-trade authority, contracting rights or government endorsement.

---

## Purpose

This document shows, in one end-to-end operating map:

- what happens;
- in what order;
- who is responsible;
- what evidence is created;
- what decision gates apply;
- when the pilot must pause;
- what outputs are generated; and
- how findings move into public-interest and government decision-making.

## Operating Principles

1. Pilot activity must follow the current governed framework.
2. Participation is voluntary.
3. EOI is not eligibility.
4. Eligibility is not competence.
5. Selection is not competence.
6. Experience is evidence of exposure, not automatic competence.
7. Unknowns remain explicit.
8. Missing evidence cannot improve an outcome.
9. Assessment does not confer statutory authority.
10. Calibration must precede live candidate assessment.
11. Safety and professional boundaries override pilot completion.
12. Material assessor disagreement must be reviewed.
13. Recruitment must not be biased toward supporters or expected passers.
14. Consent and publication consent remain separate.
15. Evidence provenance must be traceable.
16. Third-party evidence must be minimised and de-identified where appropriate, subject to unresolved legal-review questions being escalated rather than assumed away.
17. Adverse and null findings must be retained.
18. Pilot success does not automatically justify regulation.
19. Government may choose no formal recognition.
20. The pilot may be paused, narrowed, redesigned or stopped.

These principles align with, and do not replace, the current governance plan, assessment framework, calibration pack, privacy plan, complaints process and evidence requirements.

## End-to-End Pilot Flow

### Stage 0 — Strategic question
- **Purpose:** Confirm that the problem being examined is a public-interest and practitioner-function question, not a predetermined regulatory outcome.
- **Responsible role:** Initiative Lead / Pilot Sponsor.
- **Inputs:** Practitioner-function hierarchy; capability model; current policy position.
- **Activities:** State the question; confirm capability-first sequencing; confirm that no statutory vehicle is predetermined.
- **Outputs:** Strategic framing for the pilot.
- **Decision gate:** Proceed to Stage 1 only if the strategic question remains capability-first.
- **Records created:** Strategy note or decision register entry.
- **Pause / stop trigger:** Predetermined statutory outcome, unsupported policy claim, or conflict with current project position.
- **Canonical source document:** `docs/practitioner-function-decision-hierarchy-v0.1.md`.

### Stage 1 — Governance readiness
- **Purpose:** Confirm that governance, privacy, consent, complaint, conflict and readiness controls are in place before recruitment starts.
- **Responsible role:** Pilot Sponsor, supported by Pilot Coordinator.
- **Inputs:** Governance plan; role matrix; privacy plan; consent form; conflict declaration; complaints process; readiness checklist.
- **Activities:** Complete readiness checklist; assign roles; confirm document readiness; identify unresolved high-risk questions.
- **Outputs:** Formal readiness determination.
- **Decision gate:** Gate 1 — Governance ready?
- **Records created:** Completed readiness checklist; decision register entry.
- **Pause / stop trigger:** Governance gap, blocking legal-review issue under Q-018 or Q-027, legal/privacy review gap, unresolved critical questions, or redesign requirement.
- **Canonical source document:** `docs/pilot-governance-readiness-checklist-v0.1.md`.

### Stage 2 — Assessor readiness and calibration
- **Purpose:** Confirm assessors can apply the assessment framework consistently before live candidate assessment.
- **Responsible role:** Calibration Chair.
- **Inputs:** Assessment framework; calibration pack; benchmark responses; score sheets; assessor declarations.
- **Activities:** Prepare assessors; require independent scoring before discussion; run calibration scenarios; record all agreement and disagreement; review material divergence, including critical-fail disagreement; form readiness recommendation.
- **Outputs:** Assessor readiness recommendation or delay recommendation.
- **Decision gate:** Gate 2 — Assessors calibrated?
- **Records created:** Calibration results; disagreement records; calibration chair recommendation.
- **Pause / stop trigger:** Material disagreement, critical-fail inconsistency, rubric ambiguity, framework revision requirement, or inability to form a defensible readiness position.
- **Canonical source document:** `docs/advanced-carpentry-assessor-calibration-pack-v0.1.md`.

### Stage 3 — Recruitment opens
- **Purpose:** Open recruitment only after readiness and calibration gates are satisfied.
- **Responsible role:** Pilot Coordinator.
- **Inputs:** Gate 1 and Gate 2 approvals; recruitment framework; approved messaging.
- **Activities:** Open multiple recruitment channels; maintain channel neutrality; record outreach; avoid supporter-only or likely-passer selection.
- **Outputs:** Active recruitment channels.
- **Decision gate:** Continue recruitment only while readiness conditions remain satisfied.
- **Records created:** Recruitment log; recruitment notices; decision register entries where required.
- **Pause / stop trigger:** Biased channel design, inaccurate messaging, governance request to pause, or participant misunderstanding of pilot purpose.
- **Canonical source document:** `docs/pilot-candidate-recruitment-framework-v0.1.md`.

### Stage 4 — EOI and screening
- **Purpose:** Receive EOIs and conduct initial screening for completeness and basic fit.
- **Responsible role:** Pilot Coordinator, supported by Eligibility Reviewer.
- **Inputs:** EOI submissions; information statement; recruitment criteria.
- **Activities:** Log EOIs; confirm submissions are complete enough for screening; exclude clearly out-of-scope applicants using documented reason codes.
- **Outputs:** Screened applicant pool.
- **Decision gate:** Move to Stage 5 only for screened applicants with enough information for eligibility review.
- **Records created:** EOI; recruitment and selection log; screening notes.
- **Pause / stop trigger:** Consent concerns, unverifiable identity, conflict concerns, or recruitment bias patterns.
- **Canonical source document:** `docs/pilot-candidate-expression-of-interest-v0.1.md`.

### Stage 5 — Eligibility verification
- **Purpose:** Apply the documented eligibility gate separately from competence assessment.
- **Responsible role:** Eligibility Reviewer.
- **Inputs:** Qualification evidence; experience evidence; portfolio summary; references; identity and practitioner history.
- **Activities:** Verify evidence; request more evidence where needed; apply only the documented eligibility outcomes.
- **Outputs:** Eligibility finding.
- **Decision gate:** Gate 3 — Candidate eligible?
- **Records created:** Eligibility record; verification notes; participant register update.
- **Pause / stop trigger:** `UNABLE_TO_VERIFY`, unresolved mandatory evidence gaps, conflict, or inability to apply criteria consistently.
- **Canonical source document:** `docs/advanced-residential-carpentry-assessment-framework-v0.1.md`.

### Stage 6 — Candidate onboarding and consent
- **Purpose:** Confirm informed, voluntary participation and separate publication consent arrangements.
- **Responsible role:** Pilot Coordinator.
- **Inputs:** Candidate information statement; consent form; privacy plan.
- **Activities:** Provide information; obtain participation consent; separately record publication consent if given; assign participant ID.
- **Outputs:** Onboarded participant or withdrawal.
- **Decision gate:** Proceed only if participation consent is complete and valid.
- **Records created:** Consent form; participant register entry; conflict declaration if relevant; recording-consent status only if Q-024 settings have been resolved for the relevant activity.
- **Pause / stop trigger:** Inadequate consent, participant withdrawal, privacy concern, or unresolved observer/recording issue.
- **Canonical source document:** `docs/pilot-participant-consent-form-v0.1.md`.

### Stage 7 — Evidence preparation
- **Purpose:** Assemble, classify and secure the evidence set to be used in assessment.
- **Responsible role:** Candidate and Evidence Custodian.
- **Inputs:** Portfolio evidence; reference evidence; metadata requirements; privacy controls.
- **Activities:** Collect evidence; classify by canonical evidence status; check provenance and verification status explicitly; minimise third-party identifiable information where appropriate; record consent or permission uncertainty for project photographs, addresses, contracts, specialist reports and homeowner/client information; escalate unresolved legal-review issues rather than resolving them by assumption.
- **Outputs:** Classified candidate evidence set with verification status explicit.
- **Decision gate:** Gate 4 — Evidence sufficient to assess?
- **Records created:** Evidence record; metadata log; access log if required.
- **Pause / stop trigger:** Missing critical evidence, privacy issue, fabricated evidence concern, or `UNABLE_TO_VERIFY` evidence materially affecting assessment.
- **Canonical source document:** `docs/candidate-evidence-requirements-v0.1.md`.

### Stage 8 — Assessment administration
- **Purpose:** Conduct the governed assessment components.
- **Responsible role:** Assessor.
- **Inputs:** Candidate evidence set; scenarios; interview structure; communication exercise; conflict declaration.
- **Activities:** Run portfolio review, scenario assessment, technical interview and consumer communication exercise; record findings and rationale.
- **Outputs:** Complete assessment record.
- **Decision gate:** Proceed to scoring only when the administered components are complete or a governed pause is recorded.
- **Records created:** Assessor score sheet; notes; rationale; incident report if needed; recording status only where permitted under resolved Q-024 settings.
- **Pause / stop trigger:** Safety issue, conflict discovered, evidence integrity concern, or process defect.
- **Canonical source document:** `docs/advanced-residential-carpentry-assessment-framework-v0.1.md`.

### Stage 9 — Critical-fail and boundary review
- **Purpose:** Apply the non-negotiable critical-fail architecture and authority boundary checks.
- **Responsible role:** Assessor.
- **Inputs:** Assessment record; critical-fail rules; decision states.
- **Activities:** Check for unsafe conduct, authority overreach, misrepresentation, major uncertainty treated as safety, and repeated failure to communicate material risk.
- **Outputs:** Critical-fail finding or no critical-fail finding.
- **Decision gate:** Gate 5 — Critical failure present?
- **Records created:** Critical-fail record where triggered; assessor rationale.
- **Pause / stop trigger:** Any critical-fail trigger, legal boundary concern, or suspected fabrication.
- **Canonical source document:** `docs/advanced-residential-carpentry-assessment-framework-v0.1.md`.

### Stage 10 — Second review where required
- **Purpose:** Independently review borderline outcomes, including material uncertainty about performance level, or critical-fail matters before finalisation.
- **Responsible role:** Second Assessor.
- **Inputs:** Assessment record; trigger reason; assessor governance rules.
- **Activities:** Independently review the record; confirm or dispute the trigger or borderline result; document rationale.
- **Outputs:** Independent second-review finding.
- **Decision gate:** Gate 6 — Second review required?
- **Records created:** Second-review record; moderation note if required.
- **Pause / stop trigger:** Continued material disagreement, missing record, conflict involving second assessor, or inability to form a reliable view.
- **Canonical source document:** `docs/advanced-carpentry-assessor-governance-v0.1.md`.

### Stage 11 — Candidate outcome
- **Purpose:** Finalise the candidate outcome using permitted labels only.
- **Responsible role:** Lead Assessor, endorsed by Second Assessor where required.
- **Inputs:** Assessment record; critical-fail review; second-review record.
- **Activities:** Determine the final outcome; prepare domain-level rationale; issue written candidate feedback.
- **Outputs:** Candidate outcome and feedback.
- **Decision gate:** Gate 7 — Candidate outcome.
- **Records created:** Outcome record; assessor rationale; candidate feedback notice.
- **Pause / stop trigger:** Incomplete rationale, unresolved review requirement, or unverifiable evidence undermining finalisation.
- **Canonical source document:** `docs/advanced-residential-carpentry-assessment-framework-v0.1.md`.

### Stage 12 — Complaint / process review if invoked
- **Purpose:** Review process fairness separately from competence and trigger re-assessment review only when warranted.
- **Responsible role:** Pilot Coordinator, with independent reviewer where required.
- **Inputs:** Complaint submission; complaint log; relevant records.
- **Activities:** Record the complaint; review process; issue finding; trigger re-assessment review only where process review records a `PROCESS_ERROR_MATERIAL` outcome under the complaints framework.
- **Outputs:** Complaint outcome and, if required, re-assessment review outcome.
- **Decision gate:** Complaint outcome applied under the complaints framework.
- **Records created:** Complaint record; decision register entry; re-assessment review record if triggered.
- **Pause / stop trigger:** Complaint raises safety, privacy, conflict or integrity issue.
- **Canonical source document:** `docs/pilot-complaints-review-process-v0.1.md`.

### Stage 13 — Pilot evidence capture
- **Purpose:** Aggregate and secure the controlled pilot record for later review, audit and evaluation.
- **Responsible role:** Evidence Custodian.
- **Inputs:** Participant records; assessment records; complaints, incident and decision records.
- **Activities:** Store records securely; control access; retain adverse, null and inconclusive findings; record provenance and publication status.
- **Outputs:** Controlled pilot evidence set.
- **Decision gate:** Continue only if record integrity and privacy controls remain intact.
- **Records created:** Access log; retention control notes; publication control notes.
- **Pause / stop trigger:** Privacy breach, access-control failure, unresolved retention issue, or compromised evidence integrity.
- **Canonical source document:** `docs/pilot-privacy-and-data-handling-plan-v0.1.md`.

### Stage 14 — Assessor variance / calibration review
- **Purpose:** Review material divergence emerging from live pilot use and decide whether recalibration is required.
- **Responsible role:** Calibration Chair.
- **Inputs:** Assessment outcomes; variance analysis; disagreement records.
- **Activities:** Compare assessor results; classify divergence; identify rubric or scenario ambiguity; trigger recalibration or framework revision where required.
- **Outputs:** Variance review outcome.
- **Decision gate:** Gate 8 — Material assessor divergence?
- **Records created:** Variance analysis; recalibration decision; framework issue log.
- **Pause / stop trigger:** Systematic divergence, critical-fail inconsistency, or validated tolerance exceeded once defined.
- **Canonical source document:** `docs/advanced-carpentry-assessor-calibration-pack-v0.1.md`.

### Stage 15 — Consumer comprehension review
- **Purpose:** Test whether consumer-facing explanation improves understanding of role and boundaries.
- **Responsible role:** Consumer-Test Facilitator.
- **Inputs:** Consumer role-comprehension test; candidate communication material.
- **Activities:** Run the test; classify outcomes; identify misconceptions and role confusion.
- **Outputs:** Consumer comprehension results.
- **Consumer-comprehension rule:** This stage tests comprehension, not satisfaction. Clear presentation cannot compensate for unsafe reasoning or boundary failure in assessment.
- **Decision gate:** Continue, revise communication controls, or pause the relevant component.
- **Records created:** Consumer comprehension results; consent records for consumer participants.
- **Pause / stop trigger:** Material misunderstanding pattern, privacy issue, or unsafe exposure risk.
- **Canonical source document:** `docs/consumer-role-comprehension-test-v0.1.md`.

### Stage 16 — Aggregate pilot evaluation
- **Purpose:** Evaluate what the pilot evidence shows at aggregate level without overclaiming policy conclusions.
- **Responsible role:** Pilot evaluation team.
- **Inputs:** Aggregate outcomes; variance analysis; consumer results; record audits; complaints and incident themes.
- **Activities:** Assess reliability, differentiation, auditability and reviewability; identify limitations and unresolved questions.
- **Outputs:** Pilot evaluation record.
- **Decision gate:** Gate 9 — Pilot evidence sufficient for evaluation?
- **Records created:** Aggregate evaluation report; limitation log.
- **Pause / stop trigger:** Evidence set too incomplete, unresolved integrity issue, or inability to form a reliable position.
- **Canonical source document:** `docs/rrc-pilot-evaluation-framework.md`.

### Stage 17 — Public-interest evaluation
- **Purpose:** Test whether validated pilot evidence supports further public-interest analysis.
- **Responsible role:** Initiative Lead / policy evaluation team.
- **Inputs:** Aggregate pilot evidence; practitioner-function hierarchy; government-facing alignment brief.
- **Activities:** Ask, but do not pre-answer, the public-interest questions; consider role clarity, proportionality, regulatory effects and unintended consequences.
- **Outputs:** Public-interest analysis.
- **Decision gate:** Gate 10 — Public-interest case established?
- **Records created:** Public-interest evaluation note; decision register entry if scope changes.
- **Pause / stop trigger:** Evidence remains too weak, regulatory framing becomes predetermined, or key unresolved government/legal questions remain blocking.
- **Canonical source document:** `docs/practitioner-function-decision-hierarchy-v0.1.md`.

### Stage 18 — Government decision pathway
- **Purpose:** Provide government with evidence for policy choice, including no formal recognition.
- **Responsible role:** Government.
- **Inputs:** Public-interest analysis; aggregate government summary; alignment brief.
- **Activities:** Consider whether any formal mechanism is justified, and if so which vehicle is proportionate; retain no-formal-recognition as a live option.
- **Outputs:** Government decision support pathway.
- **Decision gate:** Gate 11 — Regulatory vehicle, if any?
- **Records created:** Aggregate government summary or government-facing brief.
- **Pause / stop trigger:** Further evidence required, legal/insurance analysis required, or no reliable basis for vehicle comparison.
- **Canonical source document:** `docs/government-registration-alignment-brief.md`.

## Decision Gates

### Gate 1 — Governance ready?
**Controlling source:** `docs/pilot-governance-readiness-checklist-v0.1.md`

Possible outcomes:
- `READY_TO_RECRUIT_LIMITED`
- `READY_WITH_CONDITIONS`
- `NOT_READY_GOVERNANCE_GAP`
- `NOT_READY_LEGAL_OR_PRIVACY_REVIEW`
- `PILOT_REDESIGN_REQUIRED`
- `UNABLE_TO_FORM_RELIABLE_POSITION`

**Effect:** Recruitment must not open unless the outcome is `READY_TO_RECRUIT_LIMITED` or `READY_WITH_CONDITIONS`. A blocking legal-review issue under Q-018 or Q-027 prevents a ready outcome.

### Gate 2 — Assessors calibrated?
**Controlling source:** `docs/assessor-calibration-results-template-v0.1.md`

Possible outcomes:
- `READY_FOR_LIMITED_PILOT`
- `READY_WITH_CONDITIONS`
- `RECALIBRATION_REQUIRED`
- `FRAMEWORK_REVISION_REQUIRED`
- `UNABLE_TO_FORM_RELIABLE_POSITION`

**Effect:** Live candidate assessment must not begin unless calibration has produced a defensible readiness position. Attendance alone does not satisfy this gate.

### Gate 3 — Candidate eligible?
**Controlling source:** `docs/advanced-residential-carpentry-assessment-framework-v0.1.md`

Possible outcomes:
- `ELIGIBLE_FOR_ASSESSMENT`
- `ELIGIBLE_WITH_EVIDENCE_GAPS`
- `NOT_YET_ELIGIBLE`
- `UNABLE_TO_VERIFY`

**Effect:** `ELIGIBLE_FOR_ASSESSMENT` is not a competence finding. `ELIGIBLE_WITH_EVIDENCE_GAPS` does not satisfy the gate until the documented gaps are resolved.

### Gate 4 — Evidence sufficient to assess?
**Controlling sources:** `docs/candidate-evidence-requirements-v0.1.md`, `docs/advanced-residential-carpentry-assessment-framework-v0.1.md`

Possible process outcomes:
- Proceed to assessment
- Further evidence required
- `UNABLE_TO_VERIFY`
- Pause for privacy, consent or integrity review

**Effect:** Missing evidence cannot improve an outcome. Assessors must not infer competence from absence of evidence.

### Gate 5 — Critical failure present?
**Controlling source:** `docs/advanced-residential-carpentry-assessment-framework-v0.1.md`

Possible outcomes:
- No critical-fail trigger identified
- Critical-fail trigger identified — second review mandatory

**Effect:** Critical fail cannot be averaged away. Strong technical performance does not compensate for a critical boundary failure. Weak-but-safe performance remains distinct from critical failure.

### Gate 6 — Second review required?
**Controlling sources:** `docs/advanced-carpentry-assessor-governance-v0.1.md`, `docs/advanced-residential-carpentry-assessment-framework-v0.1.md`

Possible outcomes:
- Mandatory second review — critical fail
- Mandatory second review — borderline outcome
- No second review trigger

**Effect:** Critical-fail outcomes must not be finalised without second-assessor confirmation.

### Gate 7 — Candidate outcome
**Controlling source:** `docs/advanced-residential-carpentry-assessment-framework-v0.1.md`

Possible outcomes:
- `ADVANCED_CAPABILITY_DEMONSTRATED`
- `CAPABILITY_DEMONSTRATED_WITH_DEVELOPMENT_ITEMS`
- `FURTHER_EVIDENCE_REQUIRED`
- `REASSESSMENT_REQUIRED`
- `CAPABILITY_NOT_DEMONSTRATED`
- `UNABLE_TO_VERIFY`

**Effect:** These are capability outcomes only. Prohibited labels such as `LICENSED` or `REGISTERED` must not be used.

### Gate 8 — Material assessor divergence?
**Controlling source:** `docs/advanced-carpentry-assessor-calibration-pack-v0.1.md`

Possible outcomes:
- No material divergence identified
- Material divergence review required
- `RECALIBRATION_REQUIRED`
- `FRAMEWORK_REVISION_REQUIRED`
- `UNABLE_TO_FORM_RELIABLE_POSITION`

**Effect:** Material disagreement must be recorded and reviewed. Systematic divergence can pause further assessment.

### Gate 9 — Pilot evidence sufficient for evaluation?
**Controlling source:** `docs/rrc-pilot-evaluation-framework.md`

Possible outcomes:
- Sufficient for aggregate evaluation
- Sufficient with stated limitations
- Insufficient evidence
- `UNABLE_TO_FORM_RELIABLE_POSITION`

**Effect:** Aggregate evaluation may proceed only to the extent supported by the evidence set.

### Gate 10 — Public-interest case established?
**Controlling sources:** `docs/practitioner-function-decision-hierarchy-v0.1.md`, `docs/government-registration-alignment-brief.md`

Possible outcomes:
- Supports further policy evaluation
- Mixed or unresolved public-interest case
- Does not establish a public-interest case for further formal recognition work
- Further evidence required
- `UNABLE_TO_FORM_RELIABLE_POSITION`

**Effect:** This remains an evaluation question. It is not answered automatically by positive pilot performance.

### Gate 11 — Regulatory vehicle, if any?
**Controlling source:** `docs/government-registration-alignment-brief.md`

Possible government paths:
- RRC
- DB-L reform / endorsement
- advanced credential
- government-selected alternative
- no formal recognition / status quo
- further evidence required

**Effect:** This is a government choice. This master model does not invent or imply legal approval.

## Role Interactions

### Simplified interaction map

| Role | Primary interaction | Key handoff / constraint |
|---|---|---|
| Initiative Lead / Pilot Sponsor | Sets strategic direction and approves major governance decisions | Does not automatically assess competence; approves pause/stop/redesign only through governed decision-making |
| Pilot Coordinator | Runs day-to-day pilot operations | Receives EOIs, manages onboarding, logs complaints/incidents, maintains controlled records |
| Eligibility Reviewer | Applies eligibility criteria | Hands verified eligibility finding to coordinator; does not determine competence |
| Assessor | Runs live assessment components | Creates score sheet and rationale; escalates safety, integrity and boundary concerns |
| Second Assessor | Independently reviews triggered matters | Must review without seeing the primary assessor's view first where independence is required |
| Calibration Chair | Oversees calibration and variance review | Determines readiness and recalibration need; does not override findings without moderation |
| Technical Reviewer | Gives specialist advice | Provides written advice only; does not determine candidate outcomes |
| Consumer-Test Facilitator | Runs consumer comprehension activity | Manages consumer testing without exposing participants to unsafe work |
| Evidence Custodian | Secures records and controls access | Maintains privacy, access and destruction controls; retains adverse and null findings |
| Privacy / Governance Contact | Handles participant privacy/governance queries | Escalates unresolved privacy issues or governance concerns |
| Observer | Observes without interference | Observation does not constitute endorsement; no decision-making authority |
| Participant | Provides evidence and undergoes assessment | May withdraw or complain; participation is voluntary |

### Initiative lead boundary

These constraints apply to the initiative lead / Pilot Sponsor, including Dan Sonsie in the current project structure. The initiative lead does **not** automatically:
- determine competence;
- resolve his own conflict;
- override assessors;
- decide his own appeal;
- suppress adverse evidence;
- override a justified pause or stop decision for convenience; or
- select only preferred candidates.

These limits align with:

## Evidence Flow

- **Source evidence**
- **Classified evidence with verification status explicit**
- **Assessment evidence**
- **Assessor rationale**
- **Candidate outcome**
- **Pilot aggregate evidence**
- **Public-interest analysis**
- **Government decision support**

### Where canonical evidence statuses are used

| Flow point | Status use |
|---|---|
| Source evidence intake | `USER_DECLARED`, `PHOTO_SUPPORTED`, `DOCUMENT_SUPPORTED`, `EXTERNAL_SOURCE`, `SYSTEM_DERIVED`, `PROFESSIONAL_REVIEWED` |
| Verification / classification | `UNKNOWN`, `UNABLE_TO_VERIFY` used where source or verification cannot be established |
| Assessment evidence set | Evidence is considered by assessors with provenance and metadata intact |
| Assessor rationale | Assessors explain what evidence was relied upon, what remained uncertain, and why missing evidence reduced confidence |
| Candidate outcome | Outcome is based on traceable evidence and rationale, not unsupported inference |
| Aggregate pilot evidence | Individual results are aggregated with adverse, null and inconclusive findings retained |
| Public-interest analysis | Uses only validated aggregate evidence, limitations and unresolved questions |
| Government decision support | Uses aggregate evidence and public-interest analysis only; no statutory claim arises from evidence status alone |

**Evidence rule:** If the repository uses a different final vocabulary in future governed updates, the controlling detailed evidence document overrides this summary.

**Evidence handling notes:**
- `PHOTO_SUPPORTED` does not by itself establish a technical diagnosis or professional conclusion.
- `DOCUMENT_SUPPORTED` does not by itself verify every conclusion contained in the document.
- `UNKNOWN` cannot improve an eligibility, assessment or policy outcome.
- `UNABLE_TO_VERIFY` remains an explicit state and may block progression or finalisation.

## Pilot Evidence Outputs

The pilot is designed to learn, at minimum:

- whether the practitioner function is distinguishable;
- whether the capability domains are observable;
- whether candidates can be differentiated;
- whether assessors apply the rubric consistently;
- whether critical boundaries are recognised consistently;
- whether consumer role comprehension improves;
- whether evidence records are auditable;
- whether assessment outcomes are reviewable; and
- whether the capability architecture creates useful public-interest information.

These are evidence questions only. They do not establish statutory necessity, market demand, insurance viability or government endorsement.

---

## What the Pilot Cannot Prove

The pilot cannot by itself prove:

- that a new statutory category is necessary;
- that RRC should be adopted;
- that DB-L reform is preferable;
- that defect rates will fall;
- that consumer costs will fall;
- insurance viability;
- economic benefit;
- workforce supply benefit;
- legal feasibility; or
- statewide representativeness.

This limitation is central to the operating model. **Successful pilot performance would justify further policy evaluation, not automatic statutory recognition.**

---

## Public-Interest Evaluation Bridge

Validated pilot evidence moves into the public-interest stage only after aggregate evaluation and limitation review.

Public-interest questions include:
- does the pilot generate a credible consumer benefit signal?
- does role clarity improve?
- does boundary recognition improve?
- is assessment reliable enough?
- is the evidence burden proportionate?
- what regulatory burden would any further mechanism create?
- would formal recognition add clarity or complexity?
- what market complexity might follow?
- how would builders be affected?
- how would engineers, building surveyors and licensed trades be affected?
- how would regulators be affected?
- what are the insurance implications?
- what are the enforcement implications?
- what unintended consequences may arise?

This section does not answer those questions. It defines the bridge from pilot evidence to policy evaluation.

---

## Government Decision Bridge

The final transition from pilot findings to government consideration is governed by the principle that evidence comes before vehicle selection.

Preferred statement:

> “Successful pilot performance would justify further policy evaluation, not automatic statutory recognition.”

Possible government paths include:
- further evidence;
- additional pilot;
- broader consultation;
- legal analysis;
- insurance analysis;
- RRC consideration;
- DB-L reform consideration;
- credential consideration;
- alternative mechanism; and
- no formal recognition.

This master model does not create, recommend or imply a legal approval result.

## Operating Summary

This master operating model is designed to be ready for consultation and adversarial review as a **working draft integration map**.

It is ready for that purpose only because it:
- integrates rather than replaces detailed source documents;
- preserves governance-before-recruitment and calibration-before-live-assessment sequencing;
- keeps eligibility separate from competence;
- makes evidence sufficiency, critical fail, second review and complaints separation explicit; and
- preserves no-formal-recognition as a legitimate outcome.
