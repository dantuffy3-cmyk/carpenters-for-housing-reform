## D03 — Common technical competence, separate role requirements

- Decision date: 7 October 2026.
- Status: Dan approved for controlled drafting.
- Previous position: the controlled draft needed a final direction on employee and subcontractor requirements.
- Approved direction: employees and subcontractors performing the same prescribed residential work meet the same technical competence standard. Additional requirements follow each role's responsibilities, authority and risk.
- Reason: competence for the work should remain consistent; additional business and contracting obligations must be justified by the role.
- Affected material: Question 6, primary role recommendation, consultation implications table and document control in the supplied Review 3 pair; role decision PDF.
- Treatment: avoid unrelated business or financial requirements for employees. Assess relevant additional requirements when a worker changes status without unnecessary repeat technical assessment solely because of that status change.
- Limits: retain individual responsibility for work quality, safety and boundary decisions, and retain registered-builder project accountability. Engineer, surveyor and licensed-trade responsibilities remain. Statutory terminology, contractual, probity and insurance requirements require legal and proportionality review. This decision does not create consumer-contracting or builder authority.
