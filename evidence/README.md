# Governed Claims and Evidence Register

## Purpose

The Victorian Carpenter Occupational Progression Claims and Evidence Register — Version 0.1 is the controlled source of truth for material claims used across:

- the website;
- policy papers;
- government submissions;
- consultation documents;
- stakeholder correspondence;
- pilot proposals;
- public statements.

This sprint establishes the governance foundation only. It does not publish a public evidence page and does not represent a completed audit of every live website claim.

## Core rule

No claim may be represented as verified unless it is supported by a current primary source or direct documentary evidence.

## Evidence discipline

- Practitioner accounts are not equivalent to legislation, regulation, official guidance or statistical evidence.
- Third-party AI output is not evidence.
- Interpretations must disclose their limitations.
- Unresolved questions must remain unresolved until the required evidence is obtained.
- Outdated sources must be superseded rather than silently retained.
- Public wording must match approved wording controls.
- Publication status is independent from evidence status.

## Public filtering

A future public version may include only records approved for publication and must exclude:

- personal information;
- sensitive project information;
- legally privileged material;
- unverified private allegations;
- internal strategy;
- draft legal interpretations;
- confidential stakeholder correspondence.
